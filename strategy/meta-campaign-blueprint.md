# Meta campaign blueprint: Four Paws Inn (local lead gen)

Written Oct 8, 2026 from research (sources at bottom) plus our own numbers. This is the standard every Four Paws Inn Meta campaign is checked against.

## Our numbers
- Current: about $1,150 a month (~$38 a day), 47 leads in 30 days, CPL about $24.56.
- Pet services benchmark: about $15 per lead. Room to improve.
- At ~11 leads a week we can never hit Meta's "50 per ad set per week." Expect "Learning limited." The fix is consolidation, not more ad sets.

## The setup
| Setting | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | Leads (Advantage+ Leads if offered) | Meta reports 10 to 14% lower CPL vs manual lead setups |
| Location | Pin on 1700 SW 137th Way, Miramar, **15 mile radius**, "people living in this location" | Clients want a 10 to 15 minute drive. 15 miles covers Miramar, Pembroke Pines, Weston, Davie, Cooper City, Hollywood, Miami Lakes without paying for the whole county |
| Audience | Broad. Age 25 to 65+. No interest stacking | The creative does the targeting now. Only narrow if data proves an age group never books |
| Placements | Advantage+ placements | More cheap inventory |
| Structure | 1 campaign, 1 ad set, 3 to 6 ads | Budget split thin = nothing learns |
| Form | Higher intent (review screen), 3 questions: travel dates, number and size of dogs, zip code | Filters junk leads without killing volume |
| Follow up | GHL intake text within minutes, Alex alert | Meta leads start colder than Google leads. Speed decides who books |
| Ad copy | Hook rules (content/video/hook-rules.md), proof badge, "your dog" language | First 3 seconds decide the CPL |
| Customer lifecycle | Leave on default. Check audience segments once | Only matters if past clients eat the budget |

## Testing (Rachel's 6 concepts x 5 hooks)
At our budget each ad needs enough money to show a signal.
- **Round 1, concepts:** 6 ads (Hook 1 of each), **$25 a day for 10 days** (~$250), separate campaign, same settings as above. Judge in this order: hook rate (3 second views / impressions), click through rate, then cost per lead.
- **Round 2, hooks:** the winning concept's 5 hooks, $25 a day for 10 days.
- **Round 3:** the winner moves into the main ad set. One ad added at a time.
- Never add more than 6 ads to one ad set.

## What "dominate" means here
Be the boarding ad every dog owner within 15 miles sees often enough to remember. That comes from: tight radius, one strong ad set, fresh hooks every 2 to 3 weeks, and organic reels warming the same people. Not from more campaigns.

## Later, when leads pass 200 a month
Switch the goal to Conversion Leads with GHL sending booked stages back to Meta (Conversions API for CRM).

## Decision 2026-10-08: optimize for Maximum number of leads, not Conversion leads

Keep every lead campaign on "Maximize number of leads." Reasons: (1) Conversion leads needs about 200 leads a month and FPI is at about 110; (2) operator test reported by Revel Loughlin (instagram.com/reel/DeOO1jKzMIW) found conversion leads did not beat maximize leads on any metric. The CAPI and lead ID work (Ripon) is still worth it, for measurement: it shows which ads produce real bookings so we can kill the ones that only produce form fills. Revisit when leads pass 200 a month for 2 months.
