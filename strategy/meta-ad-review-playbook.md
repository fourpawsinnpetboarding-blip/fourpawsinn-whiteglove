# Meta ad review playbook

How Four Paws Inn reviews Meta ad performance. Run weekly, and after any 7 day creative test. Source: Ben Heath, "AI Just Changed Facebook Ads Forever!" (https://youtu.be/RTrRKB0iXwQ), adapted to our account.

Update this table each review. A creative "wins" when it beats the current CPL over at least 7 days.

## The two Meta tools to use

### 1. Meta AI business assistant
In Ads Manager, click the purple and pink swirl icon.

Ask it, in this order:
1. "How do I lower cost per lead in the New Dog Ad campaign?"
2. "Which ads show creative fatigue?"
3. "What are the best performing hooks in this account?"
4. "Which creative formats should I test next?"

It also fixes account problems (payment errors, "WhatsApp number required," disapprovals). Try it first before digging in manually.

### 2. Ads Creative Studio
Ads Manager, then the three lines menu, then **All tools**, then under **Advertise**, **Ads Creative Studio**. It may not be in our account yet. Check each review.

- **Improve creatives:** why an ad misses Meta's minimum performance threshold, compared against top ads in our category.
- **Minimum performance thresholds:** the exact click through rate and cost per result Meta expects for image and video ads in our account. Record them in the baseline table.
- **Scale top creatives:** top ads with spend, CPL trend and frequency, plus video hook rate, hold rate and ThruPlay.

## Weekly review checklist

1. Pull spend, leads, CPL per ad (Claude can pull this through Windsor).
2. Per ad, check **frequency** and **CPL trend**. Frequency climbing while CPL rises means fatigue.
3. For video ads, check **hook rate** (people who watch the first 3 seconds) and **hold rate** (people who keep watching). A low hook rate means change the opening. A low hold rate means change the middle.
4. Ask the business assistant the four questions above.
5. Decide per ad: keep, iterate, or pause.
   - **Keep:** beats baseline CPL.
   - **Iterate:** close to baseline, or a winner showing fatigue. Keep the body, swap the first 3 to 5 seconds (new hook). Meta itself recommends this.
   - **Pause:** clearly worse than baseline after 7 days.
6. Log the decision and the new baseline in this file.

## Rules for AI suggestions from Meta

- **We are the filter.** Take recommendations that fit the business. Ignore the rest.
- **Allowed:** new hooks, subtitles, text on image changes, resizing for placements, animating our own review graphics.
- **Not allowed:** any generation that creates or changes dogs, people, our home, or yards ("swap product," "change visual elements," "try a new style" on photos of real dogs). Real animals and places only come from real footage. Same rule as `fourpawsinn/business-facts.md`.
- No health claims, safety guarantees or medical outcomes in any ad copy.
