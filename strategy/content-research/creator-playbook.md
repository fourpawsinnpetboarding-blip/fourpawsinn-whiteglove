## 1. Hooks: steal a proven structure, never invent one

Source: Devin Jatho (@devinjatho, 487K), "30 Viral Hooks" carousel and the reel showing 4 videos at 1.4M to 3.7M views on the same structure.

Six hook families. Each line below is already rewritten for Four Paws Inn. Fill brackets with real facts only. Experience fact (Alex, Oct 8 2026): 15 years caring for dogs. Four Paws Inn the business is 2 years old, so say "15 years with dogs", never "15 years in business".

**Cost narration** (we paid the price so you don't)
1. It took us 15 years to learn how to bring a new dog into a group. Here it is in 30 seconds.
2. After 15 years of caring for dogs, here is what I wish every owner knew before their first trip.
3. We have cared for [X] dogs. These are the 3 things that make a first stay go smooth.

**False statement** (say the myth, then flip it)
4. Never board your dog... unless you want to actually relax on your trip.
5. Home boarding is not a good choice for every dog. It is the best choice for dogs who [trait].

**Comparison**
6. Kennel boarding vs home boarding.
7. This is how boarding used to work. This is how it works here.
8. The only difference between a dog who loves boarding and one who hates it is [thing].
9. Do you want a place to drop your dog, or a place your dog wants to go back to?

**Warning**
10. Most owners skip this one thing before boarding, and that is why the first night is hard.
11. If you are boarding your dog this holiday, do not ignore these 3 rules.
12. Stop packing [item] for your dog's stay. Do this instead.

**Callout**
13. If your dog hates boarding, it is not because they are a bad dog. It is because [reason].
14. Everyone tells you to do a trial stay. Nobody shows you how. Here it is, step by step.

**Bold statement**
15. Boarding your dog for dummies.
16. If I had 7 days to get my dog ready for their first stay, this is exactly what I would do.

**Relatable hooks** (Kerbie, @kerbies.world, dog creator, 11K followers in one month)
The rule: the dog is the visual, the human feeling is the hook. Do not describe what they are watching. Give them a reason to relate.
17. They said boarding would be stressful. Nobody mentioned my dog would not want to leave.
18. POV: it is 7 AM on vacation and you already watched your dog's video twice.
19. Packing list: passport? Charger? Sunscreen? Personally, I packed my dog's Ziploc meals first.
20. I have decided checking my dog's daily video 5 times is actually a healthy habit.

Rules: no health, safety, or outcome promises in any payoff. No dashes in any line.

## 3. Formats that grew a dog daycare to 959K followers

Source: Ruff and Puff (@ruffandpuffyyc, Calgary dog bus, 959K followers, 321M total views). Their top posts are pure entertainment with named dogs. No selling in the video.

| Their format | Views | Four Paws Inn version |
|---|---|---|
| Named dog with a personality ("This boy loves his treats and friends") | 33.8M, 33.0M | One regular dog, named, with a running trait. Same dog returns in later posts. |
| Before vs after the park ("Crazy to sleepy") | 8.5M, 8.1M | Morning zoomies vs evening nap pile. Same angle, same spot, both clips. |
| Do the dogs pick their own seats? (Part 1, 2, 3) | 9.9M | Who claims which bed or couch spot. Make it a numbered series. |
| Dog birthdays ("Four birthdays on the bus") | 8.7M, 6.1M | Birthday on the stay. Ask owners for birthdays at booking. |
| Dog pouts when a friend leaves | 7.3M | Two dogs who became friends, one goes home. |
| Puppy day | 19.8M | Puppy guests together in the yard. |

Captions: ask the audience a question ("Who's dog is treats over everything?") or answer one they keep asking ("You guys always ask if...").

Mix rule: about half of reels are pure entertainment (reach and brand), half are the education and proof formats in sections 1 and 2 (bookings).

## 4. Make every post findable (search beats hashtags)

Source: Devin Jatho, "Hashtags are dead" (6.79M views, his top post).

1. Reels: before posting, add 3 topics that describe the video (Animals first).
2. Photos and carousels: fill Alt Text with keywords: dog boarding Miramar, home dog boarding Pembroke Pines, cage free dog boarding Broward.
3. Captions: keyword rich first line. Name the place and the service in plain words.
4. Keep 3 to 5 hashtags only, to categorize.

## 5. On screen layout (the "Devin Zone")

Source: Devin Jatho (929K views).

1. Bottom right "no no square": nothing goes where the like, comment, and share buttons sit.
2. Eyes (person or dog) in the upper third of the frame.
3. Captions directly under the chin or the dog's head, never at the very bottom.
4. One focal point at a time. Move the viewer's eye on purpose.
5. Quality matters: good light and a clean background. His 5.15M view reel shows the same hook failing with room light and a phone.

## 6. Profile that converts

Source: Devin Jatho, profile tiers (1.46M views).

1. Name field: Four Paws Inn | Miramar Dog Boarding (name plus a keyword people search).
2. Bio: one line pitch, proof (215 Google reviews, 4.9 stars), one call to action with the booking link.
3. Highlights: Reviews, A Day Here, Pricing, Meet and Greet.
4. Pin 3 posts: the offer explained, the best review or story, the post that brought the most followers. Do not pin by views alone.

## 7. Comment keyword to DM (DRAFT, needs Alex approval before it goes live)

Every Devin post ends with "Comment [WORD]" and an automated DM sends the thing. Comments also tell Instagram the post is worth showing to more people.

Tool: GoHighLevel, not ManyChat (Alex, Oct 8 2026: FPI does not use ManyChat). GHL workflow trigger "Instagram / Facebook comment on post" with keyword DATES, then a reply in the comments and an Instagram or Facebook DM. The commenter lands in GHL as a contact, so the normal follow ups and Alex's alerts run.

Post ending (caption and last frame): Comment DATES and we will send you our open dates.

GHL DM (sent automatically when someone comments DATES):
> Hi! Thanks for reaching out to Four Paws Inn 🐾
> Send us your dates here and we will check our openings: https://four-paws-booking.vibepreview.com
> Dogs under 50 lb are $60 a day. Dogs over 50 lb are $75 a day.
> Questions? Just reply here and Alex will get back to you.

Public comment reply (GHL): Sent you a DM! 🐾

Form leads then land in GHL and the normal follow ups run.
