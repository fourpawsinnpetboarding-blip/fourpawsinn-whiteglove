# Ad types for lead gen: Revel Loughlin (Oct 16 2026)

Source: https://www.instagram.com/p/Dei-cheE31D/ (@revelloughlin, 27K followers, runs lead gen ads weekly). Read via Eden.

## His list

Skillshow, talking head, split screen, static plus video, us vs them, phone call testimonial, obvious, bold statement, notes app, statistic static, sketchbook, written review talking head, visual hook talking head, static satire, identity contrast, inception, and hybrids.

## His 3 rules

1. Video that teaches before it asks wins lead gen. Talking heads, sketchbooks and skillshows build trust before the lead, so people pick up the phone.
2. Statics still work, but statics to a lead form bring cheap leads who never answer. He sends statics to a VSL (video sales letter: a page with one video that sells before the form).
3. Test 2 or 3 types at a time. One ad per ad set, $50 a day, 4 days each. Keep what works, kill the rest.

## The warning that matters for us

His rule 2 matches our own data. Of 25 Won bookings since Oct 8, only 5 came from Meta lead forms. Static Reviews brings leads at $16.65 each, but CPL (cost per lead) is not CAC (cost to get one paying client).

What we do instead of a VSL (our sale is a $180 to $400 stay, not a high ticket call):
1. Judge the statics on cost per booked stay, not cost per lead. Lead response Test 2 already scores contact rate and booking rate (strategy/operating-system/lead-response-tests.md).
2. Switch the instant form to Meta's "Higher intent" form type (adds a review step before submit). Fewer leads, more who answer.
3. Later, once the booking site captures click data (Codex row), test statics to the booking page against statics to the form.

## Budget reality

His "one ad per ad set, $50 a day, 4 days" means $200 per ad. 25 ads that way is $5,000 and 100 days at our spend. Too slow.
Our version: one ad set per type group, 5 groups, about $15 a day each, 7 days. Groups: Reviews, Proof and authority, Sketchbook, Notes and compare, Real photo. The winning group gets the budget, then its ads get split out one per ad set.
