# Nightly research system (Alex, Nov 6, 2026)

Alex: "I want to know how much you're researching, what you're researching, what data you're pulling, and how you're implementing it in Four Paws every single night."

## Nightly quota (every night, 10:17 PM ET)
| # | What | Target per night |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Posts and videos studied with real numbers (views, outlier score, transcript or on screen text read) | 10 |
| R2 | Creators or brands studied (their best posts vs their baseline) | 3 |
| R3 | Newsletters or email campaigns studied (from the Research label in hello@, or the brand's public archive) | 2 |
| R4 | Ads studied (Eden ads library, run days and variations) | 3 |
| R5 | Ideas implemented or queued (hook bank, a Codex Request row, a text or email draft, a reel fix) | 3 |
Weekly: 70 posts, 21 creators, 14 emails, 21 ads, 21 ideas used.

## Rotation (niche by weekday, platform mix every night)
| Night | Niche | Why it transfers |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Human parenting (daycare, nannies, summer camp, toddler sleep, separation anxiety) | Same fear: leaving someone you love with a stranger |
| Tue | Dog trainers and behaviorists | Education hub content, authority |
| Wed | Luxury hospitality (hotels, spas, resorts, pet programs at luxury hotels) | Premium rituals and wording |
| Thu | Premium local services (med spa, luxury real estate, private chefs, pediatric dentists) | How local premium businesses earn trust |
| Fri | Premium pet brands and boarding franchises (Wild One, Farmer's Dog, Ollie, Dogtopia, Camp Bow Wow, Rover) | Direct category, ads and emails |
| Sat | Sales and marketing teachers (Hormozi, Cole Gordon, Matt Ryder, Jeremy Haynes, Gary Vee) | Sales scripts, ad structure |
| Sun | Email week: newsletters and email campaigns from all of the above | Email program E1 to E3 |
If a niche runs dry (two nights with no new idea), swap it for a new one and log why. Candidates: wedding planners, pediatricians, senior care, kids sports academies, luxury car detailing, boutique fitness.

Platforms: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Threads, Substack through Eden (social index, ranked against each creator's own baseline), Meta ads through the Eden ads library, Google through web search. At least 3 platforms a night.

## The log (how Alex sees it)
Every night writes strategy/research/log/YYYY-MM-DD.md:
1. Studied: every post, creator, email and ad, with link and numbers.
2. Data pulled: the pattern (hook type, length, format, offer, CTA), with the numbers behind it.
3. Lessons: 3 at most, each one sentence.
4. Implemented: exactly what changed in Four Paws tonight (file, Eden row id, post id, text or email draft).
5. Quota check: R1 to R5 actual vs target.
The Monday scoreboard adds the weekly totals.

## Rules
- Research never changes live ads or budgets, never publishes, never picks content topics on its own (Codex leads content: ideas go to Codex as Request rows).
- No health claims, no dashes in anything client facing.
- Usage pacing: strategy/operating-system/weekly-goals.md. If usage is over 60% by Thursday, the nightly run does R1 and R5 only.
