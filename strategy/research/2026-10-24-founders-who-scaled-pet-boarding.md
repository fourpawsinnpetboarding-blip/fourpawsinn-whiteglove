Alex: "Find a business owner who was where I am (one location, local service) and scaled to multi location or a million dollar exit. I want to know the move at this level so I can act with certainty."

## What the public record shows
| Founder | Start | Before location 2 | Scale and outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mike Gould, Hounds Town USA | 2001, one 1,200 sq ft store, Port Jefferson NY | Outgrew it and leased the space next door to double it | 2nd location 2004, franchising 2012. Mature units average about $894K a year (2019) |

| Amy Nichols, Dogtopia | 2002, Tysons Corner VA, 9,000 sq ft | n/a | About 250 franchised units; average unit about $700K, top units $1.4M |
Exact revenue in each founder's early years is not public. These are the closest verifiable markers.

## The pattern
1. They maxed location 1 before adding location 2 (Hounds Town doubled its space first).
2. Location 2 came 1 to 3 years in, once location 1 ran without the founder in daily operations.
3. A mature single unit in this industry does $700K to $900K a year.
4. Growth after that was mostly buying underperforming facilities and fixing them (Pawville), not building from scratch.

## Exit math (broker data, not audited deals)
- Single location: about 2.5x SDE (seller's discretionary earnings, profit plus the owner's pay). Four Paws Inn today: about $240K a year x 2.5 = about $600K.
- Multi location group with $750K+ adjusted EBITDA: about 3.5x to 5.8x. Example: $750K x 4.5 = about $3.4M.
- Buyers are active: Trivest's Pet Resort Hospitality Group, General Atlantic's Village Pet Care, Access Holdings' Wagway.

## The math for Four Paws Inn right now
- 60 dog capacity. At about $65 a day average, full is about $117K a month.
- $30K a month is about 15 dogs a night on average, roughly 25% full.
- 50% full = about $58K a month. That is the breakthrough year: location 1 at half capacity or better for 12 months, run by someone other than Alex and Amanda.
- Ready for location 2 when: 12 months at 50%+ occupancy, a manager runs daily operations, SOPs are written, margin holds.
