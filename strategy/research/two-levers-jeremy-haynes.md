# Two levers (Jeremy Haynes reel, sent by Alex Oct 14 2026)

Source: instagram.com/reel/Deew2mPFlRH (Jeremy Haynes, 6.3K views). Claim: every growth problem is one of two things. 1) Fill the closers' calendars. 2) Add closers faster. Simplify to the lever, then get more data.

## Four Paws Inn version

| Lever | What it means here | Number to watch weekly |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Fill the calendar | Qualified leads who send dates: Meta lead ads, Google, content, referrals, events (YMCA Oct 24) | New leads with dates per week |
| 2. Closing capacity | Who replies and converts those leads, how fast | Lead to deposit rate, and minutes to first reply |

Right now lever 1 is the constraint (CLAUDE.md section 5). Lever 2 is Alex and Amanda by hand, which caps growth once lead volume rises. The speed to lead test (Oct 13 to 26) measures lever 2.

## Rule

Before starting any new project, name which lever it moves. If neither, it waits.
