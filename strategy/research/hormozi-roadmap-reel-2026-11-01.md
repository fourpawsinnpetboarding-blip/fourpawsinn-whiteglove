## Why it works
1. **Big promise hook:** "How I would start over if I had $0." A full roadmap, not a tip.
2. **Levels:** Level 1 Fundamentals, Level 2 Scale, Level 3 Keep your advantage. Viewers stay to see the next level.
3. **Every idea is a picture:** a 2x2 (Core Four), a short list (5 reasons, value levers), an acronym (CLOSER). Saves come from that: 43.9K saves, because people want the cheat sheet.
4. **One look:** flat single color background, white bold type, one simple icon per idea. Cheap to make, instantly his.
5. **Comment keyword CTA:** trades the roadmap for a comment. Comments push reach and build a lead list.
