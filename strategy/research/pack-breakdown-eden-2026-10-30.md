# Why "dog reading" videos win: Eden research (Oct 30, 2026)

Alex asked for three things, all researched in Eden: (1) why the reference reel works, (2) the same for the creator's other posts, (3) the same for similar content on other platforms. Style guide: content/video/styles/pack-breakdown.md.

## 1. The reference reel
@packleaderdogs (Steve Del Savio, 786K followers), https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcUO8DQPsZS/
- **Numbers:** 715K views, 40.5K likes, 279 comments, 96 seconds. His reels average about 193K views, so this one did about 3.7x his normal.
- **Why it works:**
  1. **The first line points at the screen:** "Look at the difference in the energy." The viewer starts watching for something right away.
  2. **One clip, real tension.** Two dogs of the same breed act completely differently. The conflict holds attention, and nothing bad happens.
  3. **A coach's voice narrating film.** He says out loud what each dog "is saying": "give me space," "you need another reminder?" Viewers feel they can now read dogs.
  4. **The lesson turns back to the viewer:** "That's who we need to be: calm, confident energy." It's about the owner, not only the dog.
  5. **The caption asks for comments:** "Are these videos helpful?" That got 279 comments.
- **His rule we don't copy:** it's someone else's footage under "fair use." We use our own clips only.

**Patterns**
1. **Reels carry the reach.** Every breakdown reel lands at 100K to 175K. The sales reel drops to 17K, so pitch rarely, and keep the pitch out of the lesson videos.
2. **"Why" titles that answer a problem owners have:** "Why other dogs correct...", "Why your dog...". Each title is a question the owner already asked themselves.
3. **Carousels are for comments, not views.** A slightly provocative title ("How to punish a dog") pulls 200+ comments. It's a conversation tool.
4. **He sells something:** an online community (Skool) plus in person training in NJ/NY. The free lessons build trust, and the paid product sits behind them. Same model as our Board and Train.

## What this means for Four Paws Inn
1. **Our closest proof is the TikTok boarding trainer:** 8M views from a normal care day narrated well. We film care days every day already.
2. **Three formats to test, in this order (Codex picks the clips):**
   - A. **Pack breakdown** (the reference): one of our clips slowed down, Alex or Amanda's voice, labels, one lesson. 45 to 90 seconds.
   - B. **A day of care, narrated:** potty rotations, intros and rest, with a voice saying what we notice about each dog. 60 to 120 seconds. TikTok and Reels.
   - C. **3 signals in 30 seconds:** two of our dogs playing, one label per signal, text only. Easy for a Trial Reel.
3. **Hooks to borrow:** "Look at the difference in..." / "Why other dogs..." / "What most people don't see is..." / "Watch his tail right here."
4. **Comments drive reach:** end with a question ("Have you seen your dog do this?").
5. **Business tie:** these build Board and Train authority. Sell once in a while, never inside the lesson.
6. **Rules stay:** our footage only, no safety claims, no breed stereotypes, never a fight, no bite stories about client dogs.
