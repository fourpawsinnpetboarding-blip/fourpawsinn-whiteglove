## Problem found: our own website shows old prices
Search results show fourpawsinn.co pages with $40, $50 and $65 a night for standard and large dogs, plus a "Premium Package" with no price. Current prices are $60 and $75. A lead who sees $40 and is quoted $60 feels baited. Fix before anything else (Codex row).

## Bundle options that fit a premium brand (for Alex's yes, later)
1. Prepaid days at the normal rate (for example 10 days, $600 for a dog under 50 lb), used any time in 12 months.
2. Clients with prepaid days get first pick of Thanksgiving and Christmas dates.
No price cut in either. Alex's rule stays: no free nights, no discounts.

Sources: rover.com Miramar and Weston boarding pages, dogvacay.com Miramar and Weston pages, campbowwow.com location pricing pages (Plymouth, Phoenix, Mansfield, Springfield East, Tampa, Summerlin).
