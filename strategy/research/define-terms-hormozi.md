# Define terms in what you can see (Hormozi, "My Process for Building Frameworks")

Source: youtube.com/watch?v=I2fnsubi9GM (MoreMozi). Sent by Alex Oct 14 2026.
Core idea: you get what you want when you can predict what happens next. You predict well when your words describe things a camera could record. Feelings and vague words ("trust", "love", "do better") cannot be acted on until they are defined as observable behavior.

## The five tools
1. Court witness test. Before a goal, request or rule counts, ask: could a witness see it happen? If not, rewrite it until they could.
2. Win, fail, measure. Before any project: what does a win look like, what does a fail look like, what number tells us which one we got, and by when.
3. Feeling to request. "I feel X" becomes "When Y happens, can you do Z?" One observable action per request.
4. Predict, then check. Write the prediction before acting. Check it on the date. The gap is the lesson.
5. Define terms first. Every SOP and every hire starts with a glossary, so two people mean the same thing by the same word.

## Four Paws Inn glossary (seed, extend in the operating manual)
| Term | Observable definition |
|---|---|
| Lead | A person who gave us a phone number or email and a reason (form, DM, call, text) |
| Qualified lead | A lead who sent travel dates and a dog that fits our intake |
| Booked | A deposit is paid and the opportunity is Won in GHL |
| Speed to lead | Minutes from lead created to our first human text or call |
| Good stay | No incident logged, daily video sent, client rebooks within 12 months |
| Done (a task) | A file exists in the repo and Alex knows his one next action |
