# Idea: sell lead reactivation to other businesses (Alex, Oct 15 2026)

Status: parked until Test 3 is scored. Do not build or sell before the proof exists (CLAUDE.md: one workstream at a time).

## The offer
Turn a business's stale CRM leads into bookings with a one to one, hand typed reactivation playbook, plus a 15 minute speed to lead standard. Proof: Four Paws Inn Tests 2 and 3 (strategy/operating-system/lead-response-tests.md).

## Niche
Start with pet service businesses: boarding, daycare, grooming, training. Same buyer, same lead pattern, and Alex is credible there. Widen to other local services only after 3 pet clients have results.

## Math to prove first
- Target from FPI: 100 old leads, about 5% book, about $300 per booking, about $1,500 recovered.
- A pet business with 500 stale leads at the same rate: about 25 bookings, about $7,500.
- Pricing to test: setup fee plus 15 to 20% of recovered revenue for 30 days, or a flat pilot.

## Gates before selling
1. Test 3 scored with real numbers (sent, replied, booked, revenue), ideally run twice.
2. Written SOP plus snippet library that a hired rep can run without Alex.
3. Texting compliance: A2P registration and consent rules for each client.
4. 3 pilot clients from Alex's network, results tracked the same way.

## Go to market, assuming Tests 2 and 3 score well (drafted Oct 15 2026)

Seller: Chiong Enterprise Inc., not Four Paws Inn LLC (keeps the boarding brand and liability separate).

Who: pet boarding and daycare owners doing $20K to $150K a month, running paid ads or Google, with a CRM (GoHighLevel first) and 300+ stale leads. Then groomers and trainers. Then other local services.

Offer ladder:
1. Free Lead Leak Audit: the same read only audit FPI ran on Oct 14 (stale leads, reply speed, who had the last word, dollars sitting). Lead magnet and sales tool.
2. Dead Lead Revival Sprint (core): 30 days, done for them inside their CRM by a trained rep, one to one, hand typed. $2,500 flat or $1,000 plus 15% of recovered revenue. Guarantee: if recovered bookings are under 2x the fee, we keep working free until they are.
3. Speed to Lead Install (continuity): saved replies, coverage schedule, weekly scorecard, staff training. $750 a month.
4. Later: the playbook as a self serve course, about $500.
