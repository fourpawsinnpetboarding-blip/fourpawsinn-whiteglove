# Reaction "case study" videos: how @packleaderdogs makes them (Eden, Oct 31, 2026)

Alex: "He's borrowing content and reacting. We can do the same." Source: Eden (his 40 top posts by views plus scripts and on-screen text). Swipe board: "Swipe: dog education that works".

## His numbers (Eden, top 40 by views)
- **Reaction case studies lead the page:** 578K (two dogs, 605 comments), 462K, 461K, 321K, 298K (551 comments).
- **Lengths run 80 to 180 seconds**, longer than most reels. People stay to hear the verdict.
- **Comments run 400 to 600 on the reaction posts.** His teaching posts get 40 to 150.

## The recipe (each step from his top posts)
1. **Source clip:** someone else's viral moment. Dog greetings, an off leash dog rushing another, a "came out of nowhere" bite, a celebrity's puppy on a red carpet.
2. **Title card on screen the whole time:** "EDUCATIONAL CASE STUDY: DOG PSYCHOLOGY & BEHAVIOR".
3. **Hook in the first line:**
   - Name the myth: "Many people would say this came out of nowhere."
   - Or point at the screen: "Look at the difference in the energy."
4. **Beat by beat:**
   - He pauses and slows the clip, and narrates what each dog "says" in the first person ("ARE YOU GOING TO ATTACK ME AGAIN?").
   - Short captions burn in on every phrase.
5. **The turn:** "This is NOT aggression. It's..." He reframes what viewers thought they saw.
6. **The lesson for the owner:** calm, confident energy. Read the warnings early.
7. **The caption ends on a question:**
   - "Did you see the warnings?"
   - "Are you getting value from these case studies?"
8. **A disclaimer:** a "Fair Use" paragraph and "Credit: Unknown".

## The catch
- He spent 6 months fighting "corporations trying to push me around, harass me, extort me" (his Oct 2026 return post). That is most likely copyright claims on the reposted clips.
- His fix: a **submission form**. Followers send their own videos with permission to be reviewed and featured.
- **Our risk is bigger.** The Four Paws Inn page runs our Meta ads and our Google listing links to it. Repeated copyright strikes on a page can limit its reach and features, and the "Fair Use" text in a caption does not stop a claim.

## Our version: same format, clips we have the right to use
1. **"Send us your dog's video" (the main source).**
   - Followers and clients send a clip of a behavior they want explained. They give permission in a simple form, and we break it down on the page.
   - Every sender is a dog owner with a training question, which makes them a **Board and Train lead**.
   - The form lives in GHL, so every submission becomes a contact.
2. **Licensed viral clips.** Clip libraries such as ViralHog or Jukin Media license viral pet videos for a fee per clip. That's a money decision, so Alex decides.
3. **Stories in the news, without the footage.** Talk about a dog story people are discussing, using text on screen and our own or stock footage. Teach the lesson, no repost.
4. **Our narration:** a calm coach Higgsfield voice (narration-standard.md):
   - fast hook,
   - a pause before each "look",
   - slow motion and a freeze frame on the key beat,
   - one to three word labels on screen.
5. **Our rules:**
   - Never film or show a fight we don't have the rights to.
   - No diagnosis or health claims.
   - Be kind about the people in the clip, like his "she isn't doing anything wrong".
   - End with a question.
