## Data pulled
- Big pet outliers this quarter are one take or near uncut, 13 to 24 seconds, with the dogs' names said out loud.
- Dogtopia's own promo reels sit below their baseline (median 0.8x); the post that broke out is a paid creator speaking the owner's guilt in first person.
- Long running pet ads are first person owner words (Chewy 85 days) or one worry, one promise ("Know more. Worry less.").

## Lessons
1. Name the dogs on camera. Names turn "dogs" into characters (malamute 113x, Farmer's Dog ad naming three dogs).
2. Uncut and calm beats edited and busy for pet content (Wally 65x). Fits the night shots Alex wants.
3. The guilt hook works best in the owner's own voice, not the brand's (Dogtopia creator reel 46x vs their promo reels 0.8x).
