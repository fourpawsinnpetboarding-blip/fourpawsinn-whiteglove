## 1. Sales: Cole Gordon (Closers.io), adapted to Four Paws
Core idea: **objection prevention, not objection handling.** If we build the right beliefs before the price, the price is not an obstacle.

### The beliefs a pet parent needs before booking (his "7 beliefs", our version)
| Belief | What the parent must feel | How we build it (texts and content) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pain | "Leaving my dog is stressful." | Ask about the trip and the worry. Name it back to them. |
| 2. Doubt | "A kennel or a random sitter won't fix that." | Ask where they stayed before and how it went. |
| 3. Cost of doing nothing | "If I pick wrong, my whole trip is ruined." | Content angle: the call to come pick up your dog mid vacation. |
| 4. Vision | "I want to enjoy my trip and see my dog happy." | The daily video, the real home, one on one time. |
| 5. Support | "My partner agrees." | Offer to send the video to their partner. Invite both to the meet and greet. |
| 6. Money | "It's worth it and I can pay." | Two totals, 3 ways to pay, the before 10 AM last day free. |
| 7. Now | "I should lock it in." | True urgency only: holiday dates book fast, the deposit holds the dates. |

### His objection buckets, our answers
- **Uncertainty** ("I need to think about it"): use his 1 to 10 scale. "On a scale of 1 to 10, how sure are you that this is the right fit for Max?" If they say 7: "What would make it a 10?" That one question isolates the real objection. Common answers: wants to see the home (meet and greet), worried about other dogs (one on one first, slow intros), never boarded (first sleepover).
- **Partner** ("I need to ask my husband"): "Of course. Want me to send you a short video you can show him?" Then book the meet and greet for both.
- **Financial** ("it's a lot"): never discount. Re explain value, offer the before 10 AM pickup (true savings), offer the 25% deposit to hold dates.

### Two more Cole Gordon rules we adopt
1. **Commit them to the process before the price.** "The way it works: I learn about Max, I tell you how his stay would go, then I give you the total. Sound good?"
2. **Get better by review.** Once a week, Claude reviews 5 real lead threads (Alex pastes or Codex exports) and marks where a belief was skipped. That is the coaching loop.

## 2. Sales: Matt Ryder (Dialer.io), adapted to Four Paws
Built into strategy/operating-system/lead-nurture-system.md. The headline rules:
1. **Speed to lead.** First touch within 5 minutes (auto text A1, approved Nov 6). Day 1 leads are worth more than day 2 leads.
2. **Most conversations happen in the first 3 attempts.** Our A stage is exactly 3 touches.
3. **Staff the hours leads arrive.** Codex is pulling our lead arrival by hour.
4. **Prioritize, don't work the list top to bottom.** Fresh first, then anyone who just replied, then fewer attempts.
5. **Follow up past "thirsty" into "respect."** His standard after a proposal: daily for 90 days, then twice a week forever. Ours (stage D): days 1, 2, 4, 7, then weekly for 8 weeks, each touch bringing something new.
6. **Know when to stop.** After 10+ failed attempts the odds are near zero (his number: under 0.1%). Move them to cold nurture, value only.
7. **Test: a call beats a text for fresh form leads.** Ryder finds texting before calling lowers the pickup rate. Test for 2 weeks: Amanda calls new form leads within 5 minutes during business hours, text only if no answer. Compare booking rates. (Alex does not take calls; this is Amanda's test if she wants it.)

## 3. Marketing: Gary Vee, applied
1. **Document, don't create.** Film the real day (drop offs, play, nap time, the daily video) instead of producing ads. This is our cheapest, most trusted content. The Friday shoot list is exactly this.
2. **Jab, jab, jab, right hook.** Give value (education hub posts) several times before asking for the booking. Matches the Oct 30 education hub plan: teaching first, offer in its own slots.
3. **Native to each platform.** Same footage, cut differently: Reels and Shorts (fast, captions), Facebook (longer caption, local), YouTube (the VSL, long form).
4. **The $1.80 strategy, local version.** Leave 90 useful comments a week (his original is 90 a day; we scale it) on local posts: Miramar, Weston, Pembroke Pines, Southwest Ranches mom groups, local dog accounts, local business pages. Helpful dog answers, never a pitch. This is a Codex job (manual work routing rule).
5. **Attention is the asset.** Go where attention is cheap: YouTube Shorts already gets 4x our Instagram views on the same clips. Post every reel there.

## 5. Ten more premium tactics
1. **A named premium tier: "Signature Stay."** Add about $20 to $25 a day on top of the normal rate. Includes the stay kit at pickup, a printed portrait, one extra photo update a day, and premium treats. It is an upgrade, never a discount, and it tests premium demand without raising the base.
2. **Anchor high first.** In quotes, show the private suite and Signature Stay before the standard rate, so the standard feels easy.
3. **Arrival and goodbye rituals.** A named greeting at the car and the stay kit at pickup. Rituals are what premium clients talk about.
4. **Target premium zip codes.** Ads and the $1.80 comments focus on nearby wealthier areas: Weston, Southwest Ranches, Davie, Pembroke Pines west, Cooper City, Parkland.
5. **Referral partners who serve the same client.** Luxury groomers, high rise condo concierges, Fort Lauderdale yacht and private aviation companies, luxury realtors (new move ins need boarding), travel advisors. Give them a card and a gift for their clients, never a discount.
6. **Client referral rewards with gifts, not money off.** A Wild One leash or a portrait session for every referred booking. Keeps the no discount rule.
7. **Premium photography.** One professional style portrait per long stay. It becomes content and the gift.
8. **Every touchpoint matches the price.** Website, welcome guide, texts, bag, the daily video quality. One cheap touch breaks the spell.
9. **Owner proof from premium clients.** Reviews and short videos from clients whose job or neighborhood signals premium (with permission).
10. **True holiday waitlist.** When holidays truly fill, open a waitlist. Real scarcity is the strongest premium signal (never invented, rule 5b).

## 7. Price raise test plan
1. **Now to Dec 31:** launch Signature Stay as an option in every quote (Alex approves the exact price and inclusions first). Track: how many choose it.
2. **Goal:** 20% of new bookings choose Signature Stay. That proves premium demand.
3. **January:** raise the base rate $5 a day for NEW clients only (returning clients keep their rate through 2027). Watch the booking rate for 30 days. If it drops less than 10%, keep it.
4. **Trigger for a bigger raise:** average occupancy above 70% for 60 days, or holidays full 3 weeks early.
