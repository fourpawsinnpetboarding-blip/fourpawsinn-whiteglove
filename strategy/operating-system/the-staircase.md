# The staircase: $30K to $1M a month

Written Oct 7, 2026, after Alex asked: "Are my goals too small? Help me think bigger."
Short answer: the goal is not too small. The order of the steps is what matters.

## The number that changes the plan

The current house can already do about $100K a month on its own.

- Capacity: 60 dogs. Price: $60 to $75 a day (business-facts.md).
- Full house for a 30 day month: 60 x $67 average x 30 = about $120,000.
- Today: about $30,000. That is roughly 1 kennel in 4 filled on average.
  (Assumes the average dog pays about $67 a day. Alex: if real occupancy is different, correct it here.)

So the first $70K does not need a new building, a loan, or a hire. It needs demand. That is why the current work is on GHL, content, and ads.

## The three steps

| Step | Revenue | What builds it | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Fill the house | $30K to $100K | GHL lead machine, GHL AI answering every lead in under a minute, content volume, Board and Train ($3,000) | 2026 to 2027 |
| 2. Sell the machine | $100K to $300K | Package the tuned GHL setup, the content engine, and the SOPs into a product other pet boarders pay for monthly | 2027 to 2028 |
| 3. Multiply | $300K to $1M | More locations (bought with SBA loans, run by operators) plus the software and media income | 2028 and on |

## Why step 2 is the big idea

A location makes money one dog at a time. Software makes money one owner at a time, and it costs almost nothing to add the next one.

- GHL has SaaS mode: a white label feature that lets you resell your own GHL setup (called a snapshot: a copy of every workflow, funnel, and template) under your own brand, at your own monthly price.
- Example math, not a forecast: 300 boarding businesses x $497 a month = about $149,000 a month, at high margin (profit kept per dollar), with no extra kennels and no extra staff to watch dogs.
- Other owners will only buy it with proof. The proof is Step 1. "We took a 60 dog home boarding business from $30K to $100K with this exact system" is the sales page.
- The Founder's Log content that documents Step 1 also builds the audience for Step 2. Same work, two payoffs.
