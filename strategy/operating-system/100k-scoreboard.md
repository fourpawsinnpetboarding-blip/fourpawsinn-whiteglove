## The predictability formula

Bookings = Leads x Qualified rate x Close rate.
Example with draft targets: 60 leads a month x 60% qualified x 40% close = about 14 new clients a month.
Once real numbers replace the drafts, this tells us exactly how much ad spend and content it takes to hit any revenue number.
