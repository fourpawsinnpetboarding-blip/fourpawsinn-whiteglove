- "Kling 3.0 7 day unlimited" appears only on **annual** Plus and Ultra. It's limited to 720p and 5 seconds, "available on web", and only if bought by Oct 21. **It does not cover our MCP workflow**, which pays credits per second. Treat it as $0 value for us.
