Alex: "We need to get to $40,000 in November. Reverse engineer how many leads we need, and get those leads."

- Booking value: about $300 average (last week $255, Aug to Oct $424, Thanksgiving stays $480 to $650).
- $40,000 / $300 = about **133 bookings** in November. October was about $18K, about 60 bookings. **Gap: about $22,000, about 73 more bookings.**

**The leads we need from Meta: about 6 a day (170 a month).** At last week's $35 CPL that costs $5,950; at Static Reviews' $19.48 October CPL it costs $3,300. Creative quality is the lever: every $10 off CPL is about $1,700 a month.

**Plain truth: ads alone do not get to $40K.** At a 10% close rate, ads alone would need about 730 leads (about $18K to $25K in spend). Reactivation is the biggest and cheapest lever, and capacity at Thanksgiving and Christmas is already tight, so early and mid November weekdays are where the room is.
