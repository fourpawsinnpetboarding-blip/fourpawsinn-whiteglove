# AI Amanda hook test, then a real shoot day (Alex, Oct 26, 2026)

Alex: "I want to use Higgsfield AI for Amanda voice and face. Test a bunch of different hooks, scripts. See what performs best, then decide in a shoot day what Amanda shoots in real life."

## How it works
1. **Build AI Amanda once.** A voice clone from her existing VSL audio (LIB-0093 to LIB-0099, about 2.8 minutes of clean speech) and her face from the same clips. Amanda listens to the voice and approves it before any test.
2. **Write the hooks.** 8 hooks a round, each the first 3 seconds of the VSL. Scripts follow business-facts.md, no "safe", no health claims. Codex reviews every script before it renders.
3. **Same body, new hook.** Every test is AI Amanda's 3 second hook plus the same 20 second real footage body. Only the hook changes, so the numbers compare the hook alone.
4. **Test quietly.** Instagram Trial Reels (shown to non followers only, never the main grid) and YouTube Shorts. No paid ads in round 1.
5. **Score after about 1,000 views each:**
   - Hook rate: 3 second views divided by impressions. The main number.
   - Hold rate: people still watching at 15 seconds.
   - Shares, saves, and DATES comments or DMs.
6. **Shoot day.** The top 3 hooks plus the VSL lines (price, reviews, next step) get filmed for real by Amanda, about 30 minutes, one session. Those real takes go into the final VSL.

## Rules
1. AI Amanda is for testing only. The final VSL, the lead follow up video and all paid ads use the real Amanda. A lead meets her at drop off; the face they trusted has to be hers.
2. Never AI dogs. The body footage is always real.
3. Amanda approves the voice clone and can stop it any time.
4. Instagram may tag the tests "AI info". That is fine for Trial Reels.
5. Credits: logged in growth-push-oct-2026.md. The standing cap is about 3 AI clips a week plus lip sync; this test needs Alex's yes on the round size below.
