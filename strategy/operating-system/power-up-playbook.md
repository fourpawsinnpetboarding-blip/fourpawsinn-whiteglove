## Four books you have not read: the juice only

### 1. The E Myth Revisited (Michael Gerber)
- **Juice:** most owners are technicians doing the work. The business only grows when you work ON it, not IN it. Build it like a franchise prototype: every task written so a stranger could run it the same way.
- **Why it matters:** this is your 2028 plan (operating manual, second location, operator partner). Your 10 month plateau is the technician trap: the business runs on you.
- **Move:** one process written per week. You talk for 5 minutes (voice note), Claude turns it into an SOP in the repo. Start with: drop off, pick up, meet and greet, new dog first day.

### 2. Traction (Gino Wickman, the EOS system)
- **Juice:** small companies stall without 3 things: 90 day Rocks (3 to 5 top priorities per person), a weekly 90 minute leadership meeting run the same way every time, and an accountability chart (one name per seat).
- **Why it matters:** you and Amanda run the business by talking all day. That works at $30K. It breaks at $60K.
- **Move:** a weekly meeting with Amanda, 30 minutes to start, same day and time. Order: scoreboard (5 min), Rocks on or off track (5 min), customer and team headlines (5 min), top 3 issues solved (15 min). Claude preps the scoreboard before.
- **Seats:** Alex = Visionary (growth, offers, ads, hiring). Amanda = Integrator of client experience (bookings, calls, staff). Claude and Codex = marketing and systems. Trainer = Board and Train.

### 3. The ONE Thing (Gary Keller)
- **Juice:** ask "What is the ONE thing I can do such that by doing it everything else becomes easier or unnecessary?" Then protect a time block for it like a meeting you cannot move.
- **Move:** this 90 days, the ONE thing is the Board and Train trainer. It adds $6,300 or more profit a month, raises revenue per dog, and is the offer that makes Four Paws Inn worth more than boarding. Your deep work blocks go here first until a trainer is signed.

### 4. Who Not How (Dan Sullivan and Benjamin Hardy)
- **Juice:** stop asking "how do I do this?" Ask "who can do this?" Your job is the result and the vision. Others own the how.
- **Move:** before you start any task, ask: is this a Claude, Codex, Amanda or trainer task? If yes, hand it off in one message and walk away. Your list should be only: decisions, money, filming, people, family.
