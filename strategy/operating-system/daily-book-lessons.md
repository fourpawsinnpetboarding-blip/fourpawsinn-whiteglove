# Daily Book Lessons

Three lessons a day, on a weekly loop, from five books: Atomic Habits (James Clear), Dopamine Nation (Anna Lembke), Tiny Habits (BJ Fogg), Indistractable (Nir Eyal), Discipline Equals Freedom (Jocko Willink).

Delivery: Google Calendar, weekly repeating events with a phone popup. 7:00 AM, 12:30 PM, 9:00 PM Eastern. Started Oct 9, 2026.

The rule behind all of it: don't rely on willpower. Design the environment so the right move is the easy one.

| Day | 7:00 AM | 12:30 PM | 9:00 PM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Every action is a vote for who you are. Act: water, sunlight, no phone before 8. (Atomic Habits) | Pleasure and pain sit on one seesaw. Act: skip the sugar, the craving passes in 10 to 20 min. (Dopamine Nation) | Never miss twice. Act: mark today on the calendar, phone to the kitchen. (Atomic Habits) |
| Tue | Start tiny. One pushup counts. Act: feet on floor, 1 pushup. (Tiny Habits) | Distraction starts inside. Act: before you open an app, name the feeling. (Indistractable) | A reset takes about 30 days clean. Act: write your sugar free day count. (Dopamine Nation) |
| Wed | Don't negotiate with the alarm. Act: feet on floor on the first ring. (Discipline Equals Freedom) | Make bad habits invisible. Act: social apps off home screen, junk out of sight. (Atomic Habits) | Plan your time or someone else will. Act: write tomorrow's Big Rock. (Indistractable) |
| Thu | Stack it: after X, I do Y. Act: after coffee, read the Perfect Day card. (Atomic Habits) | Celebrate right after. Act: say "That's me" after each win. (Tiny Habits) | Put distance between you and the trigger. Act: phone in the Kitchen Safe. (Dopamine Nation) |
| Fri | Discipline is freedom. Act: hardest task first. (Discipline Equals Freedom) | Do hard things on purpose. Act: cold water or a hard 10 min walk. (Dopamine Nation) | You fall to your systems. Act: lay out clothes, food, phone away. (Atomic Habits) |
| Sat | Make a pact. Act: tell Amanda one rule for today. (Indistractable) | Environment beats willpower. Act: remove one temptation from the house. (Atomic Habits) | If it's hard, make it smaller. Act: shrink any habit you missed this week. (Tiny Habits) |
| Sun | The weekend counts. Act: train before the house wakes. (Discipline Equals Freedom) | Traction or distraction? Act: did the last hour move you toward the man you want to be? (Indistractable) | Review the week. Act: 3 lines: what worked, what slipped, the plan. (Atomic Habits) |

To change a lesson: edit the repeating event in Google Calendar (all events), and update this table.
