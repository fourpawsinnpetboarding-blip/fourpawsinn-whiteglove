# Ad sequence playbook: what to show first (Nov 6, 2026)

Alex's question: if we can control which ads a person sees first, what order do the big players use? Attention, education, value, proof, scarcity?

## What the experts say (sourced)
- **Jeremy Haynes:** make content for every stage: awareness, nurture, conversion. A lead who opts in gets resold by the algorithm to every competitor unless we keep educating them (https://youtu.be/nC5MWa6cfGQ). He also says to assume every lead researches you between booking and showing up, and to shape what they find and answer the obvious objections before they ask (Instagram, @jeremy, Sep 2026). His agency's model: after opt in, hit the lead on every channel (email, text, retargeting) so they arrive at the call already convinced (Megalodon "Hydra" and "Hammer Them", agency claims).
- **Alex Hormozi:** every piece is Hook, Retain, Reward. Cold people get curiosity and education, warm people get proof and authority, hot people get the offer and urgency (secondary summary of his books, verify).
- **Cole Gordon world, high ticket funnels:** send a 7 to 10 minute video (VSL) after booking and before the call, so the prospect shows up pre sold.
- **Revel Loughlin (retargeting structure):** run retargeting as its own campaign with your best cold creative, at about 20% of cold spend. Watch frequency (how many times one person sees your ads) over 4 days. Above 5 to 6, lower the budget instead of scaling.
- **James Leary (lead forms):** one question on the instant form ("Are you free for a call in the next few minutes?") plus showing your number to save, raised one client's pickup rate from 50% to 80% (his claim).
- **General retargeting guides:** don't show the same ad over and over, move people through phases; short windows (7 to 14 days) for decision ads, longer (30 to 90) for nurture; 50% video watchers are a strong interest signal; local audiences are small (500 to 5,000).

None of them publish one fixed order with test data. The order below is the consensus of the above, adapted to us. We test it, we don't assume it.

## The order: Attention, then Teach, then Prove, then Remove the fear, then Ask

| Step | Who sees it | Job | Four Paws ad | Window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Attention (the trigger) | Cold: local pet parents | Stop the scroll with an emotional truth they already feel | "The suitcase is packed. You're still worried about your dog." Real drop off footage. | Always on |
| 2. Teach (value) | Watched 50% of step 1 | Show how we do it and why. No ask. | One on one time before introductions; why we keep drop off windows | 30 days |
| 3. Prove | Watched step 2, or visited the site | Other parents saying it | Static Reviews (our proven winner), owners reading reviews, the daily video | 30 days |
| 4. Remove the fear | Filled the form, last 14 days | Answer the top worries before they ask | Shy dog, other dogs, "what if he doesn't settle", first day at their own pace (Fri Nov 6 reel), the VSL | 14 days |
| 5. Ask with true urgency | Filled the form, last 14 days | Meet and greet invite, holiday dates | "Come see the home on Tuesday." "Holiday dates book fast." Only when true (rule 5b) | 14 days |
| 6. Prepare | Booked (deposit paid) | Show up calm and excited, no cancel | What to expect at drop off, what to pack | Until drop off |
| Exclude | Booked and past clients | Stop paying to reach people who already said yes | CLIENTS list | Always |

Where each idea fits:
- **Attention / trigger:** step 1 only. Emotion first (leaving your dog), never a price.
- **Education and value:** step 2, and inside every later step. This is the nurture gap Jeremy Haynes warns about.
- **Proof:** step 3. Our strongest asset (231 reviews, 4.9 stars, Static Reviews already wins).
- **Objection killers:** step 4. Answer what they research between inquiry and booking.
- **Scarcity:** step 5 only, only when true, near holidays. Never invented.

## What we run (inside the $40 a day cap)
Our warm audiences are small, so we don't run 6 ad sets. Start with 2 layers:
1. **Cold (already running):** First Sleepover challenger plus Static Reviews (paused Nov 4, returns only with Alex's yes). Step 1 and 3 creative.
2. **Warm (new, Nov 12, option A, $5 a day moved from the challenger):** one ad set, audiences LEADS90 + V1 (50% video watchers) + SITE30, excluding CLIENTS, audience expansion off. Three ads in order of the steps: one on one time (teach), owners' reviews (prove), first day at their own pace (remove the fear) with a meet and greet ask. Meta rotates them; the step order is approximate.
3. **When warm audiences pass about 1,000 people:** split into two ad sets: video watchers (steps 2 to 3) and form leads 14 days (steps 4 to 5).
4. **Frequency guard:** if 4 day frequency on the warm ad set goes above 5, lower its budget.

## The lead form tweak (test, Codex)
Add one question to the "Fall and Holiday Boarding 2026" instant form: "Can we text you in the next few minutes?" (yes or no). Show our number, 754-971-0792, to save. Yes answers get the A1 text first. Measures whether reply rate rises.
