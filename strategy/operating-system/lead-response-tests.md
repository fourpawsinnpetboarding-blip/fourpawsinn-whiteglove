# Lead response tests 2 and 3 (Alex briefing, Oct 15 2026)

Source: GHL read only audit, Oct 14 2026. This file is the locked definition. Do not change definitions after results come in.

## Audit baseline (Oct 14)
- 505 leads in New Lead stage.
- 64% got some reply within 15 minutes (automation).
- About 16% ever got a reply typed by a person inside GHL.
- Pass bar: 80% answered within 15 minutes with 3+ touches. Sample of 30: 77%. All 505: 56%.
- 107 leads sent the last message and got no answer. 8 recent leads got no reply at all.
- Stages stale: about 11 booked, 117 should be Qualified, 364 should be Lost.
- Replies from the Instagram app or a personal phone do not show in GHL. Calls are rarely logged.

## Test 2: speed to lead (replaces the Oct 13 to 26 call test)
- Population: every new lead created Oct 16 to Nov 15 2026, any stage.
- Scored: Nov 17 2026.
- Rule: a person replies inside GHL within 15 minutes, 8 AM to 8 PM, 7 days. Leads after 8 PM: reply by 9 AM. Auto replies do not count. No conversation ends with the lead's message.
- Targets: person within 15 minutes, 35% to 80%. Lead had the last word, 21% to under 5%. Also track lead to booked (deposit paid, Won in GHL).
- Only work done inside GHL counts (texts, calls through the GHL dialer, logged calls).

## Test 3: reactivation (separate from Test 2)
- 100 old leads: 18 waiting on our reply, 41 future trip, 41 went quiet.
- One to one, typed by hand inside GHL. Never bulk. 8 AM to 8 PM only.
- One message plus one follow up after 3 days, then stop. Stop or no means nothing more.
- Track per lead: sent date, replied, booked, booking value.
- Scored 14 days after the last send.

## Rules
1. Customer messages: 3rd grade reading level, short, warm with a reason, no dashes.
2. No promises about a dog's health, safety or medical outcome.
3. One to one only. No lists, no blasts.
4. No faking: auto replies never count as human, no definition changes after results, no cherry picking.
5. Reply and call inside GHL.
6. No texts before 8 AM or after 8 PM.
