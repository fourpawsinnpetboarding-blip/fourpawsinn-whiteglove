# Playing a bigger game: the 3x math (Nov 1, 2026)

Alex: "I've spent the same amount on Meta since day one. My goal is 3x revenue. Do I need to play a bigger game?"
Answer: yes. But the data says the multiplier is lead to booking, then budget.

## Data (GHL opportunities via Windsor, created Aug to Oct 2026)
- 142 won bookings in 3 months. Average booking value: **$424**.
- Won by source: Google (Google, Google Ads, Google Organic) 59; Meta (Meta, Facebook Lead Form, Facebook, Meta Ads) 38; social media 13; other 32. **Google brings more bookings than Meta.** Google spend is not connected to Windsor, so its cost is unknown.
- Meta leads that became tracked bookings, by month created:

| Month | Meta leads | Won | Booked value | Meta spend |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aug | 80 | 11 (14%) | $6,080 | not pulled |
| Sep | 66 | 5 (8%) | $1,430 | not pulled |
| Oct | 83 | 4 (5%) | $1,535 | $2,130 |

- Caveats: 128 Facebook Lead Form opportunities are still open (many are holiday dates); some Meta bookings get relabeled at won; repeat stays are not counted. True Meta return is higher than shown, but nobody can prove by how much.

## The 3x math
- Revenue about $30K a month. 3x is about $90K: about 140 more bookings a month at $424.
- 60 dogs at about $65 a day full = about $117K a month. 3x needs about 77% average occupancy. Capacity is there.
- Meta leads cost about $22. Ad spend needed for 140 extra bookings:

| Lead to booking rate | Leads needed | Meta spend a month |
|---|---|---|
| 5% (October) | 2,800 | about $62,000 |
| 10% | 1,400 | about $31,000 |
| 20% | 700 | about $15,400 |

Close rate is the multiplier on the whole game. At 5%, more budget burns cash. At 20%, $15K a month buys 3x.

## The plan
1. **Measure cost per booking, not cost per lead.** Every won booking keeps its first source tag (Codex in GHL).
2. **Work the 128 open Meta leads now.** Holiday date reactivation, drafted for Alex to send by hand. Free revenue already paid for.
3. **Lift lead to booking from 5% toward 20%.** Reply within 5 minutes, the sales order, a day 1, 3 and 7 follow up.
4. **Scale rule:** while Meta cost per booking stays at or under **$100** (about a quarter of a $424 booking), raise budget 20 to 30% every 3 days, Alex's yes each step. No ceiling but that number and capacity.
5. **Look at Google.** It already wins the most bookings. Connect Google Ads to Windsor so we can see its cost per booking.
