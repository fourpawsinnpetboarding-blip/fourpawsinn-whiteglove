# Hook test: curiosity gap plus emotion (Alex, Oct 24, 2026)

Alex: "Moving forward, I want emotion and a curiosity gap in all of my hooks. Five reels a week, every day at noon, each reel very intentional. Same exact strategy for two weeks."

## The rule for every hook from now on
1. **Emotion:** name a feeling a dog parent already has: guilt about leaving, worry their dog is bored, joy at the hello, fear their dog is too shy, pride in who their dog is.
2. **Curiosity gap:** open a question the first 3 seconds do not answer. The answer lands on the last clip before the end card, so people watch to the end.
3. Talk to the viewer ("you", "your dog"). One line, 10 words or fewer. Real footage only. No health or safety claims, no dashes.
4. Every reel: hook frame with the proof badge, middle clips build, last clip pays it off, end card. Card style cover with the same question.
