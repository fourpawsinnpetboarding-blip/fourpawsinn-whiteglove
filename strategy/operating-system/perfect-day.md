# The Perfect Day scorecard (Alex)

Defined Oct 8, 2026. A perfect day is 6 yes answers. Inputs only, things Alex controls. Outcomes (revenue, leads) are scored weekly on the scoreboard, not daily.
Built to work on any schedule, including Monday and Wednesday carpool days.

| # | Check | Yes means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Big Rock** | The one task picked last night is done or shipped |
| 2 | **No lead on read** | Every lead and client message got a human reply before bed |
| 3 | **One demand rep** | One action that puts Four Paws Inn in front of new people or moves a lead closer: film clips, approve or post content, ask for a review or referral, launch or fix an ad, follow up a warm lead |
| 4 | **Body** | 30 minutes or more of movement (yoga, run, gym, a walk counts) |
| 5 | **Present** | One block with Amanda or the kids, phone away (dinner, carpool, bedtime counts) |
| 6 | **Shutdown** | Tomorrow's Big Rock is chosen and written in Things before bed |

- 6 of 6 = perfect day. The streak counts consecutive perfect days.
- 5 of 6 = good day. Streak resets, no guilt, start again tomorrow.
- Compare to your own last week, never to Musk or Hormozi. They also started with one location and no team.

## Picking the Big Rock
The night before, ask: "Which one task, if done tomorrow, moves the 100K scoreboard the most?" Revenue and demand first, then removing Alex from operations.
