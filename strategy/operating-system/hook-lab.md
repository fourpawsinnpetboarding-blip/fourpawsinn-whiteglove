## 2. Our version: Four Paws Inn series (real footage only)
1. **"The Regulars"**: one named regular per episode with their quirk ("Bruno only sleeps on the green bed"). Same characters come back. Owners share their own dog.
2. **"Who sleeps where"**: our version of the bus seats. The nightly bed pick, filmed every night.
3. **"Before vs after pool day"**: zoomies at noon, snoring at 4 PM.
4. **"Pickup reunion"**: the dog spots its family. Most shareable moment we own.
5. **"Birthday at Four Paws"**: dog birthday during a stay. Owner shares it.
6. **"Day 1 vs Day 3"**: a shy new dog, then the same dog in the pack. Shows our slow intros without saying a word.
7. **"What happens after you drive away"**: the hour after drop off, real footage. Pure curiosity.

## 3. Pattern interrupts (Alex's dining booth idea, done with our footage)
The booth video worked because the angle was one nobody films. Our versions:
- **Dog's eye POV**: GoPro or phone at dog height running into the yard. The viewer becomes the dog.
- **"Security camera" look**: wide, high corner shot with a timestamp style text ("2:14 PM, Tuesday"). Feels like a peek nobody was meant to see. Real home camera footage only.
- **Overhead**: straight down on the pack napping in a circle.
- **The reveal pan**: start on one thing (an empty bowl, a leash on a hook), pan to the payoff (a whole pack waiting at the door).
- **Mid-action first frame**: the video starts in the middle of the jump into the pool, never before it.
- **Text you have to read**: a long on-screen first line that makes people pause to read ("POV: you told your dog you'd be back in 5 days and he heard 5 minutes").

## 4. Hook rules (every post)
1. Frame 1 is the most exciting second of the clip, moved to the front. Never a logo, a slow pan, or a blank text card.
2. Frame 1 has a word hook on screen: a question a dog parent asks, a funny truth, or a named dog's "problem".
3. Captions burned in. Works with sound off.
4. The caption ends with an easy question about THEIR dog ("Does your dog have a favorite spot?").
5. Every reel is part of a series or starts one.

## 5. Footage: what we can and cannot use
- **Use:** our own footage (FPI-VIdeos), licensed stock from sites that grant business use (Pexels, Pixabay, Storyblocks), and trending audio from the Instagram library.
- **Never:** download or repost other creators' videos. It is copyright infringement, and Meta's originality rules push reposted content down in reach. A borrowed clip costs us more reach than it gains.
- Stock is for B roll only (a suitcase, an airport, a calendar). Every dog on screen is ours.
