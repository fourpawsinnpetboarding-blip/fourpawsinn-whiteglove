# Google Ads audit (Alex, Oct 25, 2026)

Alex: "Google is our lifeline, so I never like to touch it. When I tried to increase it, I didn't see major results. We're already number one in a lot of areas for dog boarding. I don't know if I'm spending more than I should. I want fresh creatives on Google."
Spend: about $3,000 a month (Alex). Claude cannot see Google Ads directly (Windsor Basic covers Meta, GHL and Google Business Profile only), so Codex exports the data.

Rule for this audit: read only. Nothing in Google Ads changes until Alex says yes to a specific change.

## The likely answer to "why didn't more budget work"
Google only shows ads when someone searches. If we already show for about 90% or more of "dog boarding Miramar" searches (impression share), more money cannot buy more searches. It only raises the price per click. The audit checks this first: **search impression share lost to budget**. If it is near 0%, extra budget was wasted and the money is better spent on Meta, where demand can be created.

## The 8 questions
1. **Impression share:** what share of eligible searches we show for, and how much we lose to budget vs to rank.
2. **Waste:** search terms with spend and 0 conversions (for example: daycare jobs, free, cat only, far cities, competitor names, "near me" outside our radius). Each becomes a negative keyword.
3. **Cost per conversion by campaign** for the last 90 days, and the monthly trend for 12 months (spend, conversions, cost per conversion).
4. **What counts as a conversion:** calls over 60 seconds, form fills, GHL bookings (offline conversion sheet). Are we counting junk (page views, short calls)?
5. **Location and schedule:** radius around Miramar, "presence" vs "interest" targeting, and hours.
6. **Local Services Ads:** spend, leads, cost per lead, and the share of the $3,000 they take.
7. **Creative freshness:** every responsive search ad (RSA, an ad Google assembles from up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions), its ad strength and last edit date, plus image and sitelink assets. Fresh headlines to add: "No holiday fees", "230+ Google reviews", "Curbside drop off", "$60 a day".
8. **Overlap with organic:** for searches where we already rank number one organically, are we paying for clicks we would get for free (brand terms like "Four Paws Inn")?
