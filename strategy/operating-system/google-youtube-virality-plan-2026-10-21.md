# Google creatives, YouTube Shorts and the Virality Predictor (Oct 21, 2026)

## 1. Virality Predictor (Higgsfield)

**What it is:** a test you run on a finished video *before* it posts. You upload the video. It predicts how people will react, second by second, and shows the result on a dashboard:
- **Hook strength:** will the first 1 to 3 seconds stop the scroll.
- **Attention curve:** where interest rises and drops.
- **Retention risk:** the exact second people are likely to swipe away.
- **Engagement and audience response:** predicted likes, comments and shares.

It costs credits per video, so it follows the one video at a time rule.

**The loop for every new credit video:**
1. Edit the video.
2. Run the predictor.
3. Fix what it flags: a weak hook gets a new first frame or text, and a drop off point gets cut or gets a graphic.
4. Re-run only if the hook changed.
5. Post.
6. At 48 hours, log the real numbers in `content/video/content-machine-review.md`: 3 second hold, average watch time, shares, saves, comments, DMs.
7. Compare prediction vs real.

**Keep or kill rule:** after 3 videos, if the predictor's ranking matches the real ranking on at least 2 of 3, keep paying for it. If not, drop it and trust the 48 hour numbers.

## 2. Google Ads creative refresh (protect the breadwinner)

Google is the most profitable channel. The rule is: **add, never replace.**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Add new real videos and images next to the old ones | Delete or pause any old asset that is rated Good or Best |
| Add to one Performance Max asset group first | Touch bids, budget, the bid strategy or conversion goals |
| Change one asset group, then wait 14 days | Change several things in the same week |
| Read the asset ratings at 14 and 28 days | Judge anything inside 7 days (that's the learning period) |
| Remove only assets rated Low for 28+ days | Upload AI dogs or AI people |

**Asset sizes Performance Max needs:**
- Landscape image 1200x628, square 1200x1200, portrait 960x1200.
- Videos: horizontal 16:9, square 1:1 and vertical 9:16, each at least 10 seconds.
- The logo stays as it is.

**Order:**
1. Codex pulls the top 10 real videos and top 15 real images (the best performers on Instagram: the review story, drop off "first day of school", candid yard footage), exports them in the 3 sizes and saves them to Drive READY TO POST / Google Ads.
2. Alex screenshots the current asset group's asset list with the ratings before anything is added. That's the backup.
3. Claude or Alex adds the new assets to one asset group. Nothing is removed.
4. On day 14 and day 28, Claude reads the ratings and the cost per conversion. Keep the winners. Remove only Low ones that have had 28 days.
5. If cost per conversion rises more than 20% for 7 straight days after the add, remove the new assets. That puts the account back exactly as it was.

## 3. YouTube Shorts cross posting (Codex)

Codex is posting every social reel to the Four Paws Inn channel as a Short. Three changes make them searchable:
1. **Title:** what the person searches plus the place. Example: "Cage free dog boarding in Miramar FL". Never the Instagram hook alone.
2. **Description:** the first line is the same search phrase. Then: "Book at fourpawsinn.co. One video update every day of the stay."
3. **Call to action:** "Comment DATES" doesn't work on YouTube (no ManyChat). Use "Send us your dates at fourpawsinn.co".

Rules still apply: no dashes, no "safe", no health claims, no dog counts.
