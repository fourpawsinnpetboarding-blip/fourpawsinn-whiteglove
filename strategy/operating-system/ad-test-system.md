## Kill rule (plain version)
Target: a lead for $20 or less (the winner does $17.78).
1. **$50 spent, 0 leads: shut it off.** At $10 a day that is 5 days. That is 2.5 times our target cost, enough to know it is not working.
2. **$100 spent and CPL over $35: shut it off.**
3. **$100 spent and CPL at or under $20: it is a winner.** It moves up (see below).
4. Between $20 and $35 after $100: one more $50 to decide.
Why these numbers: under about 2 to 3 times the target cost, a zero is just bad luck. Past it, the ad is the problem. This is the rule Alex asked for: "$50 and not one lead, shut it down."

## Going hard (November and December)
1. A test that proves itself (rule 3) moves to its own budget and climbs 20% every 3 days while CPL stays at or under $20.
2. The winner climbs the same way once October is over, with Alex's yes on the monthly total.
3. A new test takes the $10 slot as soon as the last one is decided. One test at a time, never five.
4. Before each step up, Claude shows Alex: spend this month, leads, bookings, and Meta plus Google total.
