# Google Search campaign test (proposed Oct 11, 2026)

## Why
- Google today is one Performance Max campaign, $90 a day budget, about $80 a day spent ($1,125 in 14 days), "Limited by budget".
- Its bidding goal was "Calls from ads" counted at any length and every call. CallRail (Jun to Oct): 535 ad calls, only 11 scored qualified. Raising the budget bought more of the same calls, which matches Alex's experience that more budget never brought more results.
- Performance Max spreads money across Maps, YouTube, Display and Search. Search shows only to people typing "dog boarding" right now: the highest intent traffic.

## The test (no extra spend)
- Move $30 a day from Performance Max ($90 to $60) into a new Search campaign at $30 a day. Total stays $90 a day.
- Run 30 days, Oct 13 to Nov 12. Judge on qualified calls and booked stays from CallRail and GHL, not Google's conversion count.

## Search campaign settings
- Name: FPI Search | Dog Boarding | Miramar
- Networks: Google Search only. Search partners OFF. Display OFF.
- Location: 15 miles around 1700 SW 137th Way, Miramar FL 33027, "Presence: people in or regularly in".
- Ad schedule: 9 AM to 8 PM, 7 days (call hours).
- Bidding: Maximize clicks, max CPC $5, for the first 14 days. Then switch to Maximize conversions once it has 15+ conversions.
- Conversions: Calls from ads (20 seconds, count One) and website form submits.
- Assets: call asset (Google forwarding number), location asset (Google Business Profile), sitelinks to the price page and booking page.
