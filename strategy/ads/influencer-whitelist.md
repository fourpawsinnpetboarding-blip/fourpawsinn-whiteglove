# Local influencer and partnership ads plan (from the Hormozi video, Oct 11, 2026)

Idea: Four Paws Inn is a very visual product (happy dogs, a real yard). Instead of only buying keywords, get local dog creators to show it, then run their best video as a partnership ad ("whitelisting") on Meta, under their name, aimed at our 15 mile area.

## Pilot (start after the Search test is live; one new channel at a time)
1. Codex builds a list of 15 dog creators who live in Broward or Miami Dade (Instagram and TikTok, 2,000 to 50,000 followers, real local dog, good engagement). Columns: handle, followers, average views, dog size, area, contact.
2. Alex picks 3. Offer: a free 2 night stay (worth about $120 to $150 at cost to us, far less than ad spend) in exchange for 1 reel plus stories, honest opinion, and permission to run the reel as a partnership ad for 60 days.
3. Required: they post with Instagram's Paid Partnership label (FTC disclosure). No scripted claims about safety or health. Their own words.
4. Best reel by views and saves becomes a partnership ad in the Meta account at $15 a day for 14 days. Judge by cost per lead and booked stays against Static Reviews ($16.49 per lead) and Drop off ($8.15 per lead).

## Budget
Experimentation budget (video lesson: profit you do not need to live on is test money): proposed $1,500 a month for 90 days across Google Search tests and this pilot. Kill any test that misses its decision rule.
