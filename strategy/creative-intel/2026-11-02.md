**Pattern: two things win. Real reviews (proof) and the easy curbside drop off (no friction).** Designed formats (newspaper, cards) lose. Real footage and real words beat design.

## 5 ad ideas for the next 2 tests
1. **Review over drop off:** a real curbside handoff clip, with one short real Google review line on screen ("She didn't want to leave"). Combines both winners.
2. **The text thread:** screen of a real "on my way" text, then Amanda walking out to the car. Hook: "This is our whole drop off."
3. **Client on camera, 10 seconds at pickup:** "Why we come back." No script. The most authentic thing we can run.
4. **The daily video, from the owner's side:** a phone getting the video update while the owner is at the airport or at work. Hook: "This is what your phone gets every day."
5. **Reunion at pickup:** the dog sees its owner at the car. Hook: "Every pickup looks like this." Pair with the review line.
