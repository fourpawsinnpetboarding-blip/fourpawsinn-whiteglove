# Pet business owner calls (Layer 2 discovery)

One call a week. Not a sales call. Goal: 10 by Dec 31, 2026. See `ai-services-thesis.md` section 6b.

Ask these 4, then listen:
1. What is the worst part of your week?
2. Where do your clients come from today?
3. What have you paid to fix that, and did it work?
4. If someone set up a system that answered every lead in 90 seconds and told your ads who booked, what would that be worth to you a month?

Rules: no client or owner personal details beyond business name and city. Do not pitch. Write their exact words.
