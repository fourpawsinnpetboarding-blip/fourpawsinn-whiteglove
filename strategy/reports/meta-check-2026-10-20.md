**What it says:**
1. Static Reviews brings half the leads (14 of 28) on 41% of the spend. It's the winner.
2. The drop off video gets the most clicks (5.6% click rate), but only 3 leads from $118. People click, then don't fill the form.
3. About 35 live ads share roughly $80 a day. Most get under $5 a week, which is too little for Meta to learn which ones work.
4. Frequency is about 1.1 (each person sees an ad about once), so nothing is worn out yet.
5. Unit math: a $20 lead with a 10.7% close rate is about $190 per booked client. Break even is $272. Profitable, but cost per lead is up 19% week over week.
