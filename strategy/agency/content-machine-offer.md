The same system that runs Four Paws Inn's content, sold to local businesses.
Owner: Alex sells. Claude edits, writes and schedules. Codex handles files, uploads and music. Drafted Oct 18, 2026.
Pairs with `strategy/agency/local-growth-playbook.md`.

## What we have already built (the proof)

1. 3 posts a day on Instagram and Facebook: a 7:30 AM static, a 12 PM reel and a 4 PM carousel.
2. Real footage only, with a shot list the owner films from (`content/video/shot-lists/broll-master-list.md`).

4. Static ads in several styles (whiteboard, review cards, receipts) built from the same footage (`scripts/static-ads/`).
5. Everything is scheduled in Eden. The owner reviews after posting.

**Why these prices:**
- Short form editing services charge about $1,000 to $3,000 a month for 12 to 20 videos.
- Content agencies that also film charge about $2,500 to $6,000 a month.
- We price in the middle and include ad creatives, so the content also feeds their paid ads.

**Our cost to deliver:**
- Edit and Publish: mostly Claude and Codex time, plus music and Eden. Margin is about 85 to 90%.

- Alex never films for clients. His time goes to sales.

## Onboarding (10 days)

1. **Day 0, sales call:** look at their Instagram and Google together. Show Four Paws Inn's grid and one reel before and after editing.
2. **Day 1, kickoff form:** logo, colors, services, prices, the 3 questions customers ask most, 5 accounts they like, and Instagram, Facebook and Google Business Profile access.
3. **Days 1 to 3, brand kit (Claude):** fonts, colors, caption style, 3 static templates, intro and outro cards, and a list of words to never use.
4. **Day 3, first shot list (Claude):** 20 shots in priority order, with the same filming specs as Four Paws Inn (vertical, 4K, 10 to 15 seconds per clip, steady, good light).
5. **Days 3 to 7, filming:** the owner films on his iPhone and uploads to a shared Drive folder, or our videographer films.
6. **Day 10:** week 1 is scheduled.
7. **Approval:** the owner approves the first 2 weeks before anything posts. After that, posts go out on schedule and he has 24 hours to flag anything.

**What we promise:** volume and quality, posted on time, every time. Views and leads are reported honestly, and we never promise a viral post.

**The ad link:** fresh creatives every month keep their ads from going stale. The goal is a lower cost per lead than their old ads. The monthly report shows that number side by side.

## Rules

1. Real footage of their real business. AI is only for graphics and motion, never fake people, places or jobs.
2. Licensed music only, with credits.
3. The client owns all raw and finished files. We keep a copy only for our portfolio, with permission.
4. No claims we can't back up: no "best in town" without proof, no guarantees, no health or safety claims.
5. Each client gets their own Eden workspace or schedule. Never post to the wrong account.
