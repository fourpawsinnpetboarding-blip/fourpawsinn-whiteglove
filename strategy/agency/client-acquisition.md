# How we find clients (agency v1)

Goal: 3 paying clients in 60 days, then 10 by month 6. Alex's time cap is 5 hours a week, all on calls. Claude and Codex do the research, lists and audits.
Drafted Oct 18, 2026. Offer: `local-growth-playbook.md`, `content-machine-offer.md`.

## The core move: the free 60 second audit

Every channel below uses the same hook. We show the owner his own Google results next to his top competitor's, in a 60 second screen recording or a one page image:

> "When someone in Miramar searches pressure washing, here is who they call. Here is where you show up. Here are the 3 things fixing that."

It costs us about 5 minutes per business (Claude builds it from public data). It proves we know the work before we ask for anything, and it makes the call about his money, not our service.

## Channels, in order

| # | Channel | Why | Who does the work | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Warm list** | People who already know and trust Alex | Alex writes the names, Claude makes each audit | First 1 to 2 clients |
| 2 | **Google Maps hit list** | Businesses with weak profiles are easy to find and easy to help | Codex builds the list, Claude makes audits, Alex calls | Steady weekly flow |
| 3 | **Walk ins and job site stops** | Owners like the pressure washer answer in person, not email | Alex, when already out | High close rate, low volume |
| 4 | **Partners** | People who already serve these owners | Alex makes 1 intro a week | Referrals from month 2 |
| 5 | **Content (Founder's Log)** | Documenting builds trust at scale | Claude drafts, Alex films Sunday | Inbound from month 3 |
| 6 | **Paid ads** | Only after 2 or 3 case studies | Claude builds | Month 4 or later |

### 1. Warm list (start here)
- Business owners Alex already knows: his own vendors (the pressure washer, landscaper, pool guy, groomer, mechanic), friends, family, church, gym.
- Four Paws Inn clients who own businesses. 230+ families, and some own local service companies. Ask Amanda to flag them.
- Offer: "I grew my own business with this system. I want 3 local businesses to try it first."

### 2. Google Maps hit list
- Search "[service] + [city]" for Miramar, Pembroke Pines, Hollywood, Davie, Weston, Fort Lauderdale.
- Services: pressure washing, cleaning, landscaping, pool, mobile detailing, handyman, roofing, painting, moving, AC repair.
- **Who goes on the list:** under 20 reviews, or no website, or an incomplete profile, or few photos, and not on page 1 of Maps. Has a working phone number and is clearly active (recent jobs or posts).
- Codex builds 20 a day into a Google Sheet: name, phone, email if public, review count, rating, website, Maps rank, and the top competitor's numbers.
- Claude writes a 3 line audit for each one.
- **Contact order:** phone call first (business line), then an email with the audit, then a Facebook or Instagram DM. **No cold texts** to cell numbers (TCPA risk). Only text after they've said yes to contact.

### 3. Walk ins and job site stops
- When Alex sees a crew working (trucks, trailers, uniforms), stop, compliment the work, hand over a card, and ask: "Do you get most of your work from Google?" Then send the audit that night.
- Carry 20 printed one page audits for the area's top services.

### 4. Partners (people who serve these owners)
- Pressure washing, pool and landscaping supply stores (bulletin boards, counter staff).
- Insurance agents and bookkeepers who serve contractors.
- Local chambers and BNI groups (one meeting a week fills a calendar).
- Offer the partner a simple thank you for every client who signs: $250 or a month of service for their own business.

### 5. Content (the long game)
- Founder's Log already exists. Add an agency thread: "I am fixing a local pressure washer's Google in 30 days. Here's day 1." Then before, during and after, with the owner's permission.
- Each case study becomes a reel, a carousel and a Substack post.

### 6. Paid (later)
- Meta and Google ads to local service owners with the free audit as the offer. Only after 2 or 3 real case studies with numbers.

## The math

| Step | Rate (estimate) | Per 200 businesses contacted |
|---|---|---|
| Contacted (call, email or DM with audit) | | 200 |
| Real conversation | 25% | 50 |
| Sales call booked | 25% of conversations | 12 |
| Client signed | 30% of calls | about 3 to 4 |

200 contacts is 10 a day for 4 weeks. Codex and Claude prepare them, and Alex makes the calls.

Weekly line in the Monday scoreboard: contacted, conversations, calls, signed.
