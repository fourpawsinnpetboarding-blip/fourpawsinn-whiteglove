# AfterManual: how the machine runs (Alex, Oct 20, 2026)

Alex's goal: a constant outreach system. Claude and Codex run outbound and delivery. Alex is the face, the closer and the final approval.

## The flow

| Stage | Who | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Find prospects | Codex | Builds a daily list of local businesses (weak Google profile, few reviews, no Apple Maps) in a Sheet |
| 2. Write outreach | Claude | Personal message per prospect: what we saw on their profile, one fix, one ask. Saved as Gmail drafts in the AfterManual inbox |
| 3. Send | **Alex** | Reviews and sends the drafts. About 10 minutes a day. Nothing goes to a business without Alex sending it |
| 4. Replies and calls | Claude flags, Alex calls | Claude reads the AfterManual inbox, flags hot replies, drafts answers and books the call. Alex runs the sales call |
| 5. Close | **Alex** | Contract and $1,500 payment |
| 6. Onboard | Client, then Claude and Codex | Client gets the Intake form and the Access guide. Form answers land in a Sheet that Claude reads. Client invites the AfterManual email as manager on Google, Apple and the rest. No passwords, ever |
| 7. Build | Codex builds, Claude reviews | Launch Checklist, one tab per platform, same process we ran for Four Paws Inn |
| 8. Approve and go live | **Alex** | Claude sends Alex a short "ready to go live" summary; Alex approves |
| 9. Report | Claude | Day 30 results report from the checklist's results tab; Alex sends it |

## The rule
Claude and Codex never talk to a client or prospect directly. Everything client facing goes through the AfterManual inbox, drafted by Claude and sent by Alex, until Alex decides to hand over sending.

## What's needed to switch it on
1. One AfterManual email address (for example hello@ on the AfterManual domain), connected to Claude's Gmail access. Every client invite and every message runs through it.
2. A separate pipeline to track prospects and clients (a Sheet now; a GHL sub account later). Never the Four Paws Inn GHL.
3. A daily outreach target. Start: 20 messages a day.
