# AfterManual YouTube: the volume engine (Oct 20, 2026)

**Alex's direction, Oct 20:**
- High volume. Start at 3 to 4 long videos a day, then build to 6 to 8.
- Each video is 4 to 12 minutes.
- Each one answers one hard, specific question a local service owner has, and goes straight to the solution.
- Break the solution down, but don't hand over every implementation detail.
- Never publish the same day it's filmed. Batch first.

Back end, not decided yet: done for you (AfterManual, $1,500 launch, then $1,000 a month), or a do-it-yourself low-ticket community (Skool or similar), or both. The channel feeds either one.

This replaces Codex's "one video, then feedback" pacing in guide 03. Codex's proof rules still apply.

## The math

| Target | Videos a week | Filming sessions a week | Videos per session |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start | 21 to 28 (3 to 4 a day) | 2 | 11 to 14 |
| Build to | 42 to 56 (6 to 8 a day) | 3 to 4 | 14 |

- A talking-head answer takes about 15 to 20 minutes to record, including resets.
- So a 4-hour session gives 12 to 14 videos.
- Two sessions a week covers the starting target.
- This only works with two formats. Screen demos take hours each, so they can't carry the volume.

## Two formats

1. **Answer videos (90% of the volume):**
   - Alex on the ZV-E10 with the teleprompter, 4 to 8 minutes.
   - One hard question, one clear answer, one Four Paws Inn example.
   - Whiteboard or a single screenshot at most. No live builds.
   - Claude writes the scripts from the question bank below, edits the videos and writes titles and thumbnails.
2. **Build videos (one a week):**
   - Screen walkthroughs, 10 to 15 minutes, like Codex's guide 01 (Zelle tracking).
   - These prove you can do the work. They follow Codex's rules: test the demo first, label sample data.

## The "give the what, sell the how" rule

Every answer video gives the full **what** and **why**: the problem, the cause, the fix, and what good looks like. It stops before the **how**: the exact setup screens, the settings, and the templates.

Close: "If you want this set up in your business, that's what AfterManual does." For now, the call to action is "comment the keyword".

Never hold back a step the viewer needs to understand the answer. Hold back the build.

## Question bank, batch 1 (40 hard questions)

Each one is a title plus the core of the answer. Every answer ties back to a real Four Paws Inn example.

### Getting found
1. **Why does my competitor with worse work get more calls?** They aren't better. Google trusts their profile more: more reviews, more photos, more activity. Show a side by side.
2. **Is Google Business Profile still worth it in 2026?** Yes. It feeds Maps, AI answers and ads. Four Paws Inn: 230+ reviews.
3. **Why am I not showing up on Apple Maps?** Most owners never claim Apple Business. Every iPhone search skips them.
4. **How do I get ChatGPT to recommend my business?** AI pulls from reviews, profiles and listings. Consistent facts everywhere.
5. **Do I really need a website if I have Google and Instagram?** Yes. One page Google can index, with the price, the area and one next step.
6. **Why is my Google profile getting views but no calls?** No photos, no prices, wrong hours, weak first photo.
7. **How many photos should my Google profile have?** Real jobs, weekly. Activity is a signal.
8. **Should I pay for Yelp?** Claim it free. The paid question depends on your industry and your numbers.

### Reviews
9. **How do I get more Google reviews without being pushy?** The ask in the last 15 minutes of the job, in person.
10. **Why automated review texts can hurt you.** Real reviews from happy moments beat a text sent 3 days later.
11. **What do I say when a customer leaves a bad review?** One calm reply, take it offline, fix the process.
12. **Is 4.9 stars better than 5.0?** People trust 4.9 with volume more than a perfect 5.0 with 12 reviews.

### Google Ads and Performance Max
13. **I spent $2,000 on Google Ads and got nothing. What went wrong?** No conversion tracking, so Google learned nothing.
14. **Performance Max vs Search ads: which should a local business run?** Alex's take, from running PMax at about 8x revenue per ad dollar.
15. **Why Performance Max loses money for the first 30 days.** The learning period, and what not to touch.
16. **What daily budget does Google Ads need to work?** Enough data to learn. Why $10 a day starves it.
17. **What is a conversion, and why is it the most important setting?** Count calls and booked jobs, not clicks.
18. **Why your Google Ads leads are junk.** Wrong conversions, no negative keywords, a weak offer.
19. **How I lost money for 2 months on Google Ads and what fixed it.** Alex's real story. Use only what Alex confirms.
20. **Should I hire an agency or run Google Ads myself?** The honest answer: what it takes in time versus money.

### Leads and speed
21. **Why speed to lead is the cheapest growth hack.** Reply in 5 minutes vs 5 hours.
22. **My leads go cold after the first text. Why?** The first reply asks for nothing. Ask one easy question.
23. **What to text a lead who ghosted you.** The "no rush" message that brings them back.
24. **Should I send prices in the first text?** When to quote, and when to qualify first.
25. **How to answer "how much?" without losing the lead.** Price plus a reason plus a question.
26. **Missed call text back: does it work?** Yes. What it should say.

### Sales and pricing
27. **How do I raise my prices without losing customers?** Raise them for new customers first.
28. **Why discounts attract the worst customers.** Four Paws Inn's no-discount rule.
29. **How to charge for a premium service in a cheap market.** Sell the outcome and the trust.
30. **What a deposit does for your business.** Fewer no-shows, and serious buyers.
31. **The 3 numbers every local owner should know.** Customer acquisition cost, average job value, and how often customers come back.

### Systems and AI
32. **How I run my business with AI and two assistants.** Claude and Codex, the Eden board, and how the handoff works.
33. **AI keeps writing the wrong prices. Here's the fix.** One fact sheet, and a human checks before anything goes out.
34. **What should I automate first in my service business?** The repeated message after payment: the welcome guide.
35. **How a checkbox in Google Sheets sends my welcome email.** Show the result. Hold back the script.
36. **Spreadsheet or CRM: which does a small business need?** Start with the sheet, move when it breaks.
37. **How to know which ad brought a paying customer.** Lead source on every booking.

### Content and owner time
38. **How to make 30 videos in one afternoon.** The Ask Amanda method.
39. **Why your content gets views but no customers.** Answer buyer questions, not trends.
40. **What to post on your Google profile every week.** Real jobs, real photos, one sentence.

## Rules for every video

- Use real numbers only when Alex confirms them. Four Paws Inn numbers are framed as "my business", not as client results.
- No income promises and no "guaranteed".
- No dashes in titles or on-screen text.
- Never show customer data on screen.
- Never name or trash a competitor.
