# Agency scale plan (Alex, Oct 18, 2026)

**Business name: AfterManual** (Alex, Oct 18, 2026). Register it as a fictitious name (DBA) under Chiong Enterprise Inc. on Sunbiz.

Alex's goal: 100 clients at $5,000 a month. Speed matters. Four Paws Inn stays the long game (national brand, $100M exit). The agency is the fast cash engine.

## Milestones

| Stage | Clients | Average a month | Monthly revenue | What has to be true |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proof | 5 | $3,000 | **$15,000** | 5 case studies with real numbers. Alex sells and does support calls |
| Traction | 20 | $3,000 | **$60,000** | First account manager hired. Free support calls replaced by a weekly group call |
| Scale | 50 | $3,500 | **$175,000** | 3 account managers, 1 videographer network, price raised for new clients |
| Goal | 100 | $5,000 | **$500,000** | 6 to 7 account managers, a sales hire, Alex runs the company, not the accounts |

## Where it breaks (and the fix)

1. **Alex's hours.** One free hour a week per client works at 5 clients (5 hours). At 20 it's 20 hours a week, which pulls Alex out of Four Paws Inn and out of sales. Fix: free 1 to 1 calls for the first 5 clients only. After that, one weekly group call for all clients, 1 hour.
2. **Delivery volume.** At 100 clients on content, that's about 1,200 to 1,600 reels a month. Claude and Codex do the editing, but QA, client messages and approvals need people. Fix: 1 account manager for every 15 to 20 clients (about $4,000 to $5,000 a month each). Margin stays above 70%.
3. **Results.** One bad client story spreads faster than five good ones. Fix: say no to businesses that can't fund at least $30 a day in ad spend, or that don't answer their phone.
4. **One per industry, per area.** This is a promise in the sales video. Track it in the client sheet so it's never broken.
