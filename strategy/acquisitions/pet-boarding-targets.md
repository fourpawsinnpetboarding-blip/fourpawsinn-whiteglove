## Screening questions before any call

1. Is boarding at least half of revenue, or is it a grooming shop with a few runs?
2. Lease or owned real estate? Lease term left, and can it transfer?
3. Is the city zoning and business tax receipt clean for overnight boarding? (Lesson from our own code compliance notice.)
4. How much of the cash flow is the owner's own labor? A 1.3x multiple usually means the buyer is buying a job.
5. Capacity, average occupancy, and daily rate versus ours ($60 and $75).
6. Reviews: count and rating. A weak reputation is fixable with our playbook and is where the upside is.
