# Educate at every stage (Alex, Nov 5, 2026)

Source: Jeremy Haynes, "Why You Need Organic Content to Make Paid Ads More Profitable" (https://youtu.be/nC5MWa6cfGQ). Alex: "This is gold. We must educate."

## The lesson in one line
The moment someone inquires or books, the algorithm learns what they want and fills their feed with every other provider. Whoever keeps teaching them between the inquiry and the drop off keeps the client. The one who only ran an ad loses them.

His story: he booked a landscaper's call off an ad. The landscaper sent a plain confirmation and nothing else. For 4 days his feed taught him everything about grass from other creators. He found a better option and never showed up.

## What it means for Four Paws Inn
1. **The gap is where we lose people.** Leads take 1 to 4 days to answer and book weeks ahead. In that gap their feed shows other boarders, Rover sitters and dog content. Our content and messages must fill that gap first.
2. **Content for all three stages.**
   - Awareness (strangers): the education hub. Training, behavior, breeds, first stays. Teach, don't sell.
   - Nurture (leads who inquired): how we do things and WHY. Arrival, first day, introductions, daily video, why the rules exist.
   - Conversion (ready to book): reviews, the offer, the booking steps.
3. **Every lead gets education, not just a confirmation.** After an inquiry: the VSL or one teaching video. After a deposit: the welcome guide plus one "why we do it this way" message before drop off.
4. **Retargeting idea (after the Nov 11 freeze, needs Alex's yes for spend):** a small budget showing nurture videos only to people who filled the form or engaged. They already raised their hand; keep teaching them.

## Explain the why behind every rule (client messages and content)
Rules without a reason feel cold. A short reason turns a rule into care. One sentence, plain words, no safety claims, no dashes. Use these approved lines:

| Rule | Why line |
|---|---|
| Drop off and pick up windows (9 AM to 12 PM, 4 PM to 6:30 PM) | We keep drop offs to set times so the house stays calm. Your dog walks into a quiet home, not a busy one. |
| Text your ETA, we meet you at the car | A calm hand off at the car helps your dog settle in, and it keeps the dogs inside relaxed. |
| Morning drop off for a first sleepover | A morning drop off gives your dog a full day to get to know us before bedtime. |
| First sleepover before a holiday stay | It lets your dog learn the house and our routine before a long stay. |
| Meet and greets on Tuesdays only | Weekends are our busiest days. Tuesdays are calm, so you get our full attention. |
| No visits during a stay | A visit can make a dog think it's time to go home. The daily video lets you see them without that. |
| Food packed one meal per Ziploc bag | So every meal is just like home and nothing gets mixed up. |
| New dogs start with one on one time | Every dog settles in at their own pace, so we start one on one before any introductions. |

Use one why line per message, where it fits. Never stack them. Never lecture. Warmth plus clarity plus reason (CLAUDE.md section 9).
