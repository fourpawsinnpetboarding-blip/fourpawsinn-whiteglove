# Engagement plan plus Higgsfield tool map (Oct 20, 2026)

## Where we are (Eden analytics, last 30 days, Instagram)
- Views rose from 1,459 to 16,758 (11x). Posting rhythm and brand look are working.
- Engagement rate is **0.63%**. Matched peers sit at **2.30%**, so we're about 3.7x lower, in the 17th percentile.
- Only 14 comments across 210 posts. Breakout rate 0%, peers 26%.
- **What beats our own average:** real review stories (Tamara's senior dog, 4.8x), emotional drop off posts ("first day of school", 3.8x) and candid real footage ("two dogs, a patch of grass", 3.1x).
- Statics and carousels (1.32x) beat our reels (0.94x).

**The read:** we look professional, but we don't **invite** anyone to respond. Every caption ends with the same line ("If you're near Miramar..."). People scroll past a sign-off they've seen 50 times.

## Engagement fixes (free, start this week)
1. **End every caption with one easy question,** not the boilerplate. For example: "What's your dog's weirdest habit?", "Pool or nap: which is your dog?", "Who else does the long goodbye at drop off?" Keep the booking line as the second to last line, short: "Booking? Send us your dates."
2. **Reply to every comment within the first hour,** with a question back. Alex or Amanda do this; it doubles the comment count and the algorithm reads it.
3. **More of what already wins:** a real review story twice a week, the parent emotion angle twice a week, and candid real footage daily.
4. **Stories every day, 3 to 5 frames:** a poll, a quiz, a "this or that", a countdown to holiday spots, and behind the scenes. Stories build replies and DMs, and DMs are leads.
5. **Carousels built to be saved:** "5 things to pack", "How we introduce a new dog", "Signs your dog is ready for the group". Saves and shares weigh more than likes.

## Tests, one at a time (the learning loop)
1. **Week 1 (free):** question captions plus reply in the first hour. Compare the engagement rate with the 0.63% baseline.
2. **Week 1 (free, web):** 2 save-bait carousels made with Nano Banana Pro. Measure saves.
3. **Week 2:** one review card with a real dog cutout (remove background) compared with a plain text review card.
4. **Week 2:** run the virality predictor on the next noon reel before it posts, then compare its prediction with the real 48 hour numbers. Keep using it only if the predictions match reality.
5. **Week 3:** daily stories with a poll. Measure replies and DMs.

**Goal by Nov 20:** 1.5% engagement rate and 50+ comments a month.
