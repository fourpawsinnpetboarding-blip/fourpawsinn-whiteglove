# Lessons: Vanessa Lau, "The exact system I used to rebrand my Instagram in 7 days" (Alex shared Nov 6, 2026)

Source: https://youtu.be/dJxlFgG7CrA (full transcript read through Eden).

## The 7 steps she used
1. **Audit the feed.** Cover the bio and look at the last 12 feed posts. Would a stranger know who you are and who it's for in 3 seconds? Strangers judge the feed, not Stories.
2. **Set content goals first.** Why are you posting? Hers: attract customers, attract the right brand partners, attract creators. Every pillar must serve a goal.
3. **Build the audience avatar as a whole person.** Their day, what keeps them up at night, what they're tired of, what they use now instead of you, what makes them hesitate, words to use and avoid, who they are NOT. "Think about the person, not the niche."
4. **Pick 3 to 4 broad pillars** where what you like making overlaps with what the avatar wants. Formats are not pillars. Too narrow and you run out of ideas.
5. **Study 5 admired creators** for patterns, not to copy:
   - Central theme: every post feeds one storyline (Grace Beverley).
   - B roll of the product while you talk about something else (Elise).
   - Repeatable weekly formats so you never start from zero (Michelle).
   - Film moments live, don't recap them later (Millie Goldsmith).
   - Series with a clear goal and a deadline raise the stakes (Julia Montgomery).
   - Visual cohesion: same fonts, same style, recognizable with the sound off.
6. **Write a content operating guideline:** theme, guardrails, weekly system, series rules.
7. **Script with frameworks.** Her diary formula: what happened this week, why it hit me, what it made me realize, what I'm trying now.

## How it applies to Four Paws (proposal for Codex, who leads content)
1. **Audit now.** Codex covers our bio and reads our last 12 feed posts: do they say "a real home for your dog while you travel, in Miramar"? Our grid has mixed dog facts, packing tips, reviews and pricing. Facts carousels and montages were our weakest posts (mission-nov-dec-2026.md).
2. **Central theme:** "Your dog's home away from home." Every post shows or teaches what life in the house is like.
3. **Avatar (the whole person):** a South Florida pet parent who travels, treats the dog like family, feels guilty leaving them, has been let down by a kennel or a sitter, and wants proof, not promises. Hesitations: other dogs, a shy dog, "will they miss me", price. Who it's NOT: bargain shoppers.
4. **Repeatable weekly formats inside our cadence** (noon feed: reels Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri, Sun; carousels Wed and Sat):
   - **Mon: This week at the house.** Amanda documents one real moment live, using the diary formula.
   - **Tue: A routine, no talking.** Drop off, breakfast, nap time, bedtime. Night shots are needed (Alex wants more dogs sleeping inside).
   - **Wed carousel: one lesson** (education hub, cited source).
   - **Thu: Amanda talks while doing a task** (feeding, packing the stay kit, the daily video). The house is the B roll while she teaches.
   - **Fri: a series with stakes.** Example: "First stay to second stay", one dog's first night to their return visit (real, with the owner's OK, no outcome promised).
   - **Sat carousel: the week in reviews and photos.**
   - **Sun: the Billion View hook format** (hook-lab.md, the crate idea).
5. **Visual cohesion:** the card cover style already does this (content/covers/README.md). Keep one font set and one caption style on every reel.
6. **Film live:** Alex and Amanda capture moments as they happen (the dog's first steps into the house, pickup reunions), not recap voiceovers.
