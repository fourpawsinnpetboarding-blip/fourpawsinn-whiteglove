Alex: "I want the page to start just focusing on giving value on dog training, because we're going to roll out the dog training option and that concept performs well on social media. Reviews and testimonials, dog education, breed education, training, puppy, elderly, everything. We're going to become the source of dog questions getting answered. People follow because they know they'll learn something about dogs, cats or exotic animals. Then we'll always have the other kind of action."

Also: "I don't want to use specific dogs in our home." We teach with general examples. We don't analyze or name client dogs.

## Why it fits the numbers
- Education breakdowns pull 100K to 8M views in Eden (strategy/research/pack-breakdown-eden-2026-10-30.md). Our care posts pulled 0.27% engagement per post (CLAUDE.md, Oct 23).
- It builds the audience that Board and Train sells to: owners with a training problem who already trust us.

## The mix (one feed post a day stays the cadence)
| Share | Pillar | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| About 60% | Dog training and behavior | Recall, leash pulling, jumping, barking, crate, "why does my dog..." |
| About 15% | Life stages and breeds | Puppy first weeks, senior comfort, what a breed was bred to do |
| About 10% | Cats and exotics | Cat stress signs, rabbit and bird basics (we board them too) |
| About 15% | Proof and offer | Real Google reviews, testimonials, Board and Train, holiday dates |
Every education post ends the same way: one follow line ("Follow for a new dog lesson every day") and a soft line about us. The hard offer goes in the proof and offer slots only.

## What Claude added, and why (one line each)
1. **Follows per post is the main number now**, with saves and shares. A teaching page wins on follows and saves, not likes.
2. **Breed education is allowed, but as history and needs, never "this breed is perfect here".** It keeps the Oct 23 rule that every dog is its own dog.
3. **Health stays out.** We teach behavior, training and daily care, and say "ask your vet" for anything medical. That's liability, not style.
4. **Facts get a source:** AKC, ASPCA, AVMA or the Humane Society, named in the caption. It makes us the trusted source and protects us.
5. **One series name per pillar** (for example "Dog Lesson #12", "Breed in 30 Seconds"), so viewers know what's coming and follow for the next one.
6. **Questions from leads become posts.** Every question in GHL ("do you take intact males?", "what should I pack?") is a ready lesson with proven demand.
