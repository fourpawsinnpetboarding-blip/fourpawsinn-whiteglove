# SOP: Dog waste, food waste and trash odor

Owner: whoever closes the house that day. Written Oct 7, 2026, after a maggot and fly problem in the cans next to the neighbor's side.

## Why it happens
Flies lay eggs on uncovered food and waste. In Florida heat, eggs become maggots in about 1 day. Loose waste sitting in a can for days = smell, flies, maggots.

## The rules
1. **Nothing goes in the can loose.** Every scoop of poop and every bit of leftover food goes in a bag, tied tight. Double bag food.
2. **Leftover food never sits.** Unfinished meals go into a small lidded kitchen bin with a liner. Empty that bin into the outside can at closing, every night.
3. **Lids stay shut.** If a lid does not close all the way, the can is too full. Use the second can or bag it for tomorrow.
4. **Every pickup day, every can goes out.** Even if it is half full.
5. **Cans live in the shade, away from the neighbor's fence**, when possible.
6. **No chemicals the dogs can reach.** For odor use baking soda in the bottom of a clean can. Fly traps go only where dogs cannot reach them.

## Daily closing check (2 minutes)
- [ ] Kitchen food bin emptied into outside can, bag tied
- [ ] Yard scooped, bags tied, in the can
- [ ] Both lids fully closed
- [ ] Tomorrow is pickup day? Cans to the curb tonight

## Weekly (Sunday)
- [ ] Hose out any can that smells, then a cup of baking soda in the bottom

## Monthly
- The Bin Father cleans the cans ($25 a month, hired Oct 7, 2026). Put cans out on their visit day.

## If it is still too much waste
Ask Miramar Public Works about adding a second garbage cart, or price a pet waste pickup service. Track cost against the neighbor risk.
