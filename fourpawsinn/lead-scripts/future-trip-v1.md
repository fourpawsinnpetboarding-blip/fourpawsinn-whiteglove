# Lead script: Future trip, not sure yet (version A)

Use for every new lead who answers "Future trip / not sure yet". Same words every time so we can measure what works.
Save each message as a GHL Snippet named exactly as below. Log every lead it is sent to in the tracking column of the scoreboard.
Facts used: `fourpawsinn/business-facts.md` (pricing, last day rule, deposit, daily updates). Adjust the price line for dogs over 50 lb ($75 a day).

## FT-A1: first reply (send within 5 minutes, same channel they used)

Hi {first name}, it's Alex from Four Paws Inn. Thanks for filling out our form! For a pup under 50 lbs it's $60 a day, and the last day is free if you pick up before 10 AM. Every guest gets daily photo and video updates. What's your pup's name? And do you have rough dates in mind yet?

## FT-A2: day 3, no reply

Hi {first name}, just checking in. Holidays fill up fast here. When you know your dates, send them over and we'll check the calendar for you. A 25% deposit holds your spot.

## FT-A3: day 10, no reply

Hi {first name}, no rush at all. Whenever your next trip comes up, just text us your dates and we'll take great care of your pup.

## Rules

- A human sends or approves every message. No AI sends.
- If they reply with dates, stop the script and quote the stay.
- Track: sent date, replied (yes or no), booked (yes or no).
