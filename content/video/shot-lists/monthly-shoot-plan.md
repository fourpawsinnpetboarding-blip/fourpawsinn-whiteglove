# Monthly shoot plan: 2 shoot days, 30 reels (Alex, Oct 22, 2026)

Alex: daily filming is not sustainable. Two organized shoot days a month, a shot list, and a month of reels planned in series. The daily client videos stay exactly as they are; we only save a copy.

## The rhythm
- **Shoot Day A:** 1st Sunday of the month. **Shoot Day B:** 3rd Sunday. About 90 minutes each, spread across the day as the dogs' schedule allows (morning release, midday pool, afternoon nap, bedtime).
- **Each shoot day = 15 reels.** Two days = 30, one per day at 12:00 PM.
- **Passive footage (no extra filming):** the daily client videos Amanda already sends. Before she deletes nothing; she drops the day's clips in Drive `FPI-VIdeos / RAW / client-daily` once a week (Sunday, 5 minutes). Those feed the "Day 1 vs Day 3" and "Regulars" series.
- Codex logs everything in the footage index. Claude and Codex each cut a version of the same series and compete on the weekly scoreboard.

## Gear and settings (once)
Phone, vertical, 4K 30 fps (120 fps for pool slow motion), a small tripod, a clip or low stand for dog height. Clean the lens. Never film into the sun.

## Shoot day shot list (in order of the dogs' day)

| # | Block (time) | Exactly what to film | How | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Morning release (8 AM, 10 min) | The back door opening and the pack running out | Phone at dog height (knee level) INSIDE the door, start recording 5 s before it opens, keep rolling 20 s | 2 reels: "POV: you're a dog at Four Paws Inn at 8 AM" |
| 2 | The Regulars (9 AM, 20 min) | Pick 3 regular dogs. For each: 3 clips of their quirk (the toy they steal, the spot they guard, the dog they always find), plus 5 s of their face looking at camera | Handheld, close, at their level | 3 reels: "The Regulars: meet [name]" |
| 3 | Pool (11 AM to noon, 15 min) | Dogs jumping and swimming. Get 3 jumps from the side, 2 from above | Start recording before the jump. 120 fps for 2 of them | 2 reels: "Before vs after pool day" (part 1 of 2) and "Pool day in Miramar" |
| 4 | Security camera (midday, 10 min) | The yard or living room with the pack, no people | Tripod HIGH in a corner, wide, leave it rolling 10 minutes untouched | 2 reels: "We checked the camera at 1:14 PM" |
| 5 | Reveal pans (anytime, 10 min) | (a) Leash on its hook, pan to the pack waiting at the door. (b) Empty bowls, pan to the line of dogs waiting for dinner | Slow, smooth pan, 6 to 8 s | 2 reels: reveal hooks |
| 6 | Nap (3 to 4 PM, 10 min) | The SAME pool dogs from block 3, asleep | Straight down overhead if possible, then close ups | Completes "Before vs after pool day" (part 2) |
| 7 | Ask Amanda (5 min) | Amanda answers 3 questions to camera, 15 s each: "Will my dog be sad?", "Does my dog sleep in a crate?", "What if my dog is shy?" | Tripod, eye level, quiet room, window light | 3 reels: "Ask Amanda" series |
| 8 | Bedtime (8 PM, 10 min) | Dogs choosing where to sleep. Wide shot of the whole room | Tripod in the corner, 3 minutes rolling | 1 reel: "Who sleeps where" |

Total: 15 reels per shoot day.

## The month in series (noon slot, one reel a day)
| Day | Series | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Ask Amanda | Shoot day, block 7 |
| Tuesday | The Regulars | Shoot day, block 2 |
| Wednesday | Before vs after pool day | Shoot day, blocks 3 and 6 |
| Thursday | POV or security camera | Shoot day, blocks 1 and 4 |
| Friday | Day 1 vs Day 3 (shy dog to pack) | Client daily videos |
| Saturday | Reveal or Who sleeps where | Shoot day, blocks 5 and 8 |
| Sunday | Best moment of the week (reunion, birthday, zoomies) | Client daily videos |

Same day, same series every week. The audience learns what Tuesday is.

## Hook for each reel (frame 1)
Every reel opens on the most exciting second of the clip with words on screen. Examples:
- POV: "POV: you're a dog and it's 8 AM at Four Paws Inn"
- Regulars: "Meet Bruno. He has one job."
- Pool: "12 PM vs 4 PM"
- Security camera: "We checked the camera at 1:14 PM"
- Reveal: "Someone said the word walk"
- Day 1 vs Day 3: "Day 1: hiding under the chair. Day 3:"
- Who sleeps where: "Every night they pick their own bed"
- Ask Amanda: "Will my dog be sad when I leave?"

## Rules
- Real dogs, real home. Licensed stock only for non-dog B roll. Never repost other creators.
- Everyone in footage Alex or Amanda sends is cleared. Never hold a video over people or consent.
- No dog counts, no "safe", no health claims, no dashes in on-screen text.
