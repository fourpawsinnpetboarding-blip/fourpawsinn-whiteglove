# B roll master shot list (ads and organic)

Owner: Alex films. Claude edits. Made Oct 17 2026 from the ad angles that are live or queued.
Drop everything in Drive: FPI Videos / RAW / [date] broll. Text Claude "uploaded".

## Specs for every clip
- Phone vertical, 4K, back camera. Hold each shot 10 to 15 seconds, steady. No talking needed.
- Also take 3 photos of every setup (stills become static ads).
- Shoot in shade or morning light. No sun behind the subject.
- Rules: no children except Alex's son (approved). No client faces or license plates unless the client says yes. No messy areas in frame. Never show a dog count or a full crowd shot of every dog at once.
