## Words we did not use, and why
- "First paw in": she was already in the water in that shot. Not true, so cut.
- "We hold on until she's ready": promised a moment the footage never shows.
- "Safe", "always", "never": safety guarantees. Not allowed.

## The standard for every reel from now on
1. Write the opening shot in one sentence before anything else. If it has no stakes or surprise without text, pick another shot.
2. Every line of text must be true of the frame it sits on.
3. The question opened in the first 2 seconds is answered on screen before the end card.
4. Check the hook frame at full size: 6 elements or fewer, text never covers the dog.
5. One reel built this way beats ten built fast. Volume only after a format is proven.
