# Shoot week of Nov 9: the 3 best videos (Alex, Nov 6: "three max, the best of the best")

Built from the research so far: Adley Kinsman's opposite action formula (1.17M views, 13.8x), @themalamuteman (3.49M, 113x), Wally uncut (23.7M, 65x), Dogtopia's owner voice creator reel (143.7K, 46x vs their promo reels at 0.8x), our own winners (real footage, reviews, the drop off Short at 4x), the pacing gate (hook by frame 1, voice by 1s, 12 to 30s) and Alex's ask for more night shots, more of us on camera, and our own dogs.

Rules for every shot: phone vertical (9:16), 4K or 1080p at 30 fps, lens wiped, 3 takes of each, hold 2 seconds before and after the action. Drop everything in Drive RAW, one folder per video (V1, V2, V3). People in footage are cleared (CLAUDE.md). No dog counts on camera or in text.

## V1. "We don't use crates." (the opposite action video)
**Why it wins:** Adley's formula (do the opposite to an object people tie to one feeling) plus our biggest difference, cage free, in one picture.
**Length:** 18 to 22 seconds. **Frame 1 text:** "We threw out the crate."
**Truth check before filming:** we must not crate dogs anywhere in the stay. If we keep any crate for any reason, use the line "Your dog won't sleep in one of these" instead.

| Time | Picture | Words |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2s | Alex carries a folded wire crate out the front door and sets it at the curb. | Text: We threw out the crate. |
| 2 to 4s | Close: Amanda drops the crate's latch pin into the Four Paws tote on the counter (the anchor). | Amanda: "Not one dog sleeps in one of these." |
| 4 to 13s | Fast cuts, 1.5s each: hand off at the car, paws stepping inside, nose on the couch, yard zoomies. | Text: A real home. |
| 13 to 19s | Night: dogs asleep on the couch and beds, slow pan. | Amanda: "This is bedtime here." |
| 19 to 22s | Pickup: the tote handed to an owner, the latch pin now on the tote as a keychain. | Text: Cage free in Miramar. Send us your dates. |

**Shot list (V1)**
1. Alex carrying the crate out, wide from the street. 3 takes.
2. Close up: latch pin dropped in the tote. 3 takes.

## V2. "Bedtime at our house." (the uncut video)
**Why it wins:** uncut and calm beats edited (Wally 65x). Names make dogs characters (malamute 113x). It fills our biggest gap: no night footage.
**Length:** 20 to 30 seconds, ONE take, no cuts. **Frame 1 text:** "Bedtime at our house."

**A roll (Amanda or Alex, with our own dogs and guest dogs):**
Phone on a tripod at couch height, lamps on, room quiet. Sit on the couch or the dog beds and talk softly to each dog by name while they settle, like you would at home. Example: "Okay Coco, you always pick the big bed. Max, you're last to settle every night. Goodnight, everybody." Do not plan it word for word. Do 3 full takes and we pick the best one.
**End text (added in edit):** Every night. A real home, not a kennel.

**Also film (for future edits), 5 to 8 seconds each:**
1. One dog asleep on a bed, close.
2. Two dogs sleeping side by side.
3. A dog stretching awake in the morning.
4. Lights turning off, the room going calm.
5. Our own dogs on the couch with Alex or Amanda, just hanging out.

## V3. "I used to feel so guilty." (the owner's voice video)
**Why it wins:** the guilt hook works when the owner says it, not the brand (Dogtopia creator reel 46x vs their own promo at 0.8x). Real owner words also run longest in pet ads (Chewy 85 days) and match our winner, Static Reviews.
**Length:** 15 to 25 seconds. **Frame 1 text:** the owner's best line, in their words.

**A roll (2 or 3 clients at pickup this week, 15 seconds each):**
At the car, after the reunion. Amanda off camera asks ONE question: "How did you feel leaving her before you found us?" Let them answer. Don't coach them.
**B roll:** the reunion itself, the dog running to the owner at the car (film from behind the dog). This is the payoff shot.
**End text:** 231 Google reviews. Real families.

**Backup if no client is free (founder version, Alex on camera with our own dogs):**
Alex sits with our dogs and says, in his own words: "We used to feel guilty leaving our dogs home alone all day. So we built the place we wanted for them." Then the yard, the group, the nap. One take, no script card.
