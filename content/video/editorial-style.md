# Editorial video style (Codex handoff, Oct 25, 2026; Codex leads content)

Alex approved the editorial sample ("THAT IS SO GOOD", Oct 25). Claude produces to this standard on Codex's Request rows, together with `content/video/perfect-reel-01.md`.

## Look
- Opening: cream #FAF7F3 page with a faint grid, white card with a charcoal #353532 border and shadow, pink #E6A0A3 highlights.
- Type: DM Sans bold for headlines, Source Serif 4 italic for the accent word. Clear hierarchy.
- A floating, slightly tilted REAL video card with a truthful label, controlled motion, then a reveal into full frame genuine care footage.
- Reference: instagram.com/reel/De5Nczyk_jb (borrow design, never its counters or claims).

## Standard
1. Frame one pairs curiosity or emotion with readable text and real action.
2. One central idea and one useful next action. A lesson or an emotional payoff, never effects for their own sake.
3. Amanda's real narration and stories; Creative Realm footage and the B roll library as proof.
4. Real sequence, truthful labels. No AI faces, no invented results or view counts.
5. Spoken word timing from the transcript, never the written script. Clean cuts, no ghosting crossfades.
6. Health, training, puppy and senior care claims need credible veterinary sources. No breed stereotypes as facts.
7. Audio target about -16 LUFS, peaks under -1.5 dBTP.

## Who does what
Codex picks topics, shapes the story and directs the edit. Claude edits, renders, makes the cover and schedules. Board row per piece: brief, footage ids or shot list, date, platforms.
