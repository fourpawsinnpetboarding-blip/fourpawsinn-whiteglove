## The job of the VSL
One video that takes a stranger from "who are these people" to "send them my dates". It has to answer the 5 worries every lead has, in this order:
1. Will my dog be ok there? (the home, the people, the pace)
2. Who is watching my dog? (Amanda, the team, the routine)
3. Will I know how they are doing? (daily video updates)
4. What does it cost, and is there a catch? (price, no hidden fees)
5. What do I do next? (send dates, optional Tuesday tour)

## What works (keep)
1. **The positioning line is the best thing in it:** big facility or someone watching dogs on the side; we built Four Paws Inn to sit in the middle. That is the hook. It is buried at 0:08.
2. **The curbside drop off with a reason:** new faces and knocking get dogs worked up, so we come to your car. A rule plus a reason builds trust.
3. **The slow start:** explore the house, then the yard, then the pack when ready. Shy dogs get their own space. This is the answer to worry 1.
4. **Small groups by size, temperament, energy and play style:** "the right pack, not the biggest pack" is a strong line.
5. **Daily updates and no hidden fees:** both answer real worries.
6. **The closing line:** "Professional enough to trust, small enough to know your dog." Keep it as the tagline.
