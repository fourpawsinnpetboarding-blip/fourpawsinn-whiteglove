## Gap shot list (film these, nothing else)

Each one is missing from the library. Phone vertical, 4K, 10 to 20 seconds, steady, daylight unless noted.

1. **Alex talking to camera, 5 short answers** (20 to 40 seconds each): What is cage free? How much is it? What do I pack? What happens on day one? Why a home and not a kennel? Founder reels have zero footage today.
2. **Amanda talking to camera, 3 answers**: How do you handle a nervous dog? What does a normal day look like? What do you send parents each day?
3. **Where dogs sleep**: beds set up, lights dimming, dogs settling at night (lamp light is fine).
4. **Morning wake up**: dogs stretching, first potty walk outside.
5. **Pick up reunion**: owner walks in, dog runs to them. Get the owner's OK. This is the most emotional shot a boarding business has and the library has none.
6. **Daily update being sent**: screen record the phone sending a photo update. Hide the client's name.
7. **Tuesday meet and greet in progress**: wide shot plus a close up of the dog sniffing the new space.
8. **Indoor calm**: dogs napping on the couch or bed, small dogs indoors.
9. **What to pack, laid out on a table**: food in labeled bags, leash, bed or toy.
10. **Close ups**: tails wagging, paws on grass, a dog running straight at the camera.

With these 10, the library covers every pillar for about 8 weeks without repeating a hero clip.
