- The story holds: the couple worries, the real staff bond with the dog, the step tracker, the phone video, the review. The same hero dog runs from start to end.

Use Allison's "Great communication from Amanda as well; would definitely recommend!" (already used in Dog Sitter) or another real Google review that names no frequency.

3. **Trim the end card from about 6 s to 3 s.** From 0:20 to 0:31, 11 seconds are static cards: a third of the video, right where people swipe. Target length: 26 to 27 s.
4. **Loudness to −14 LUFS** at the final export.

## Dog Sitter Bailed, Cut B: 25.9 s. Score 22/30 (was 17). Hold until Tiny Dictator has 48 hours of numbers.

**What works**
- Hook: a real dog runs at the camera, with "Vacation planned. Dog sitter bailed? Trip: 7 days". Clear problem in 1 second.
- Middle: the MEET, SNIFF, SETTLE, FRIENDS tracker over real staff footage, with a "Miramar, FL" tag. That's the proof the old cut lacked.
- The end card has no black frame.

**Fixes (required)**
1. **One dog, one "he".** The hook dog is a brown and white mix. "He ate her bed" shows the dachshund. The middle shows 4 other dogs. "He needed a vacation from us" shows a Doberman. Viewers can't follow whose story it is. Pick one hero dog (the Doberman has the payoff shot) and use him in the hook, the bed line and the ending. Other dogs can appear only as "other dogs" in the FRIENDS step.
2. **Replace the 0:16 couple background.** Sea cliffs and Mediterranean water read as fake and as "not Miramar". Use a living room or patio. A see-through phone rectangle also overlaps the man during the transition: cut straight to the phone instead.
3. **Fix the audio balance.** −20.5 LUFS overall with a 10 LU range means some parts are much quieter than others (likely the narration against the music). Duck the music under the voice, then bring the whole video to −14 LUFS.
4. **Don't reuse the dachshund.** She's the star of Tiny Dictator. The same dog in both ads makes the second one feel recycled.
