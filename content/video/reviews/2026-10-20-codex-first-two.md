## The rule question (Alex decides)

Both videos use **AI dogs** as the guest dog. business-facts.md says Four Paws Inn dogs, animals, clients and the home must come from real footage. The AI couple works as clearly fictional skit actors. An AI dog shown "staying with us" makes viewers think a real guest stayed here.

- **Option 1 (recommended):** the AI couple stays fictional. **Every dog on screen is real footage** of a real guest. Add a small "Dramatization. Real dogs, real home." label.
- **Option 2:** keep the AI dogs for the at-home skit scenes only (never at Four Paws Inn), and add the label.
