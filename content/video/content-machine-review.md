# Content machine: video review and enhance process (Claude)

Owner: Claude reviews and finishes. Codex builds first cuts. Alex approves.

## Handoff (every video)
1. Codex uploads the **full quality file** to Drive, IN PROGRESS, in a dated folder.
2. Codex adds an Eden board Request row, Owner Claude, with: the Drive link, the script, which clips are AI and which are real, the music and its license, and the credits spent.
3. For a quick look, Alex can send the file straight into Claude chat. The final edit always works from the Drive original.

## Review scorecard (1 to 5 each, 30 points total)
1. **Hook (first 2 seconds):** a clear question, tension or pattern break? Is it readable with the sound off?
2. **Real proof:** does real footage, a real review or a real transformation carry the claim? Is AI only there to support it?
3. **Value:** does the viewer learn one useful thing (what happens on day one, how slow intros work, what to pack)?
4. **Story and entertainment:** is there a beginning, a turn and a payoff? No dead seconds?
5. **Visuals:** a cut every 1 to 3 seconds, captions in brand colors (cream, pink, charcoal), the logo, licensed music with credit?
6. **CTA (call to action):** one clear ask, "send us your dates"? Does it match the noon reel slot?

**Under 20:** rebuild. **20 to 25:** Claude enhances. **26 and up:** ready for Alex's review.

## Hard rules
- No AI dogs, people, home, yard or clients presented as real. An AI character is always clearly fictional or stylized, and it's cut around real footage.
- Real reviews are quoted word for word, with first name only, from Google.
- Transformation stories are used only when Alex or Amanda confirms they're true.
- No "safe", no health claims, no dog counts, no discounts, no dashes in on-screen text or captions.
- Every reel passes the QA in scripts/video-engine/weekly-run.md before it's scheduled.

## Credit log
Log credits per video in strategy/operating-system/higgsfield-credit-budget.md, so the 30-day measurement is real.

## The learning loop: one credit video at a time (Alex, Oct 20)

1. **Brief:** write down the hook, the story, the real proof, the CTA, and **one thing we're testing** (for example, an AI cold open compared with a real footage cold open).
2. **Build:** generate only the shots the script needs. Estimate the credits first; if the estimate passes 120, Alex approves.
3. **Score:** use the scorecard above. Ship it only at 26 or more.
4. **Post** in the noon reel slot.
5. **Measure at 48 hours and 7 days:** 3 second hold, average watch time, completion rate, shares, saves, DMs and leads.
6. **Lesson:** write one line in the log below: what worked, what failed, and what changes in the next video.
7. **Next video:** change **one thing at a time**, so we know what moved the numbers.

**Ready for volume when:** 3 videos in a row score 26 or more, beat our real footage reel average on watch time, and land within the credit estimate. Then Alex decides.
