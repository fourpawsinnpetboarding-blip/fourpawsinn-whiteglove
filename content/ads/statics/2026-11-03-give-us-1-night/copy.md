# AD52 to AD54: "Give me 5 days" layout, Four Paws Inn version (Nov 3, 2026)

Alex, Nov 3: "I want this specific ad worked on for us." Reference: Adley Kinsman's "GIVE ME 5 DAYS. Double your reach" static (Viralish, Nov 2 to 6 challenge), running at 6,649% more views than her typical post.

## What we kept from her ad
1. A short, bold promise as the headline ("Give me 5 days" becomes "Give us 1 night").
2. One line saying who it is for ("For brands that sell online" becomes "For dogs who have never boarded").
3. A second bold line with the result ("Double your reach" becomes "See them settle in").
4. One pill with the details, a founder credit, a real smiling person in a rounded frame, and one giant button.
5. Highlight bars behind the key words, in our pink, not her green.
