# AD73 to AD80: First Sleepover, FINAL COPY (v4, Nov 4, 2026)

This is the only copy to use. Older drafts are in copy-history.md and are retired.

## What is on every image
- Four Paws Inn logo and name, top left.
- Enlarged review badge: 5 stars, "230+ GOOGLE REVIEWS". No decimal rating on the image (see Rating check).
- Amanda in every photo (only 2 verified photos of her exist, so each is used in 2 crops).
- Pill: "FIRST NIGHT FROM $60 • MIRAMAR".
- Conditions strip: "$60 a night: dogs under 50 lb, pick up before 10 AM. Other rates apply."
- Button: "BOOK THEIR FIRST SLEEPOVER".
- Meta headline (all 8): Give your dog a first sleepover. 230+ Google reviews.
- Meta button: Get quote. Lead form: the current one.

## Rating check (Nov 4)
Pulled all 232 Google reviews from the Business Profile API: 229 five star, 1 two star, 2 one star. Average 4.95. Google shows one decimal on the live profile, and that display was not checked, so no number goes on the ads. If someone sees 5.0 or 4.9 on the live profile, it can be added to the badge.

## Wording rule for this set
The ads push a first night hard before the holidays but never say it is required, and never promise it reserves or guarantees a holiday spot. (How new dogs are handled for holiday stays is a sales step, not ad copy.)
