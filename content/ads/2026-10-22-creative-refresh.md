**Lessons**
1. The winner looks like a native local listing (dense, navy, review cards, map, icons). The 9 clean cream brand templates got almost no delivery. On Meta, proof that looks native beats polished brand design.
2. Drop off ("pull up, text us, we bring your dog to your car") is the winning message. New hooks on it lost; the message didn't.
3. Copy issues found in the live ads (not fixed yet, Alex decides):
   - Static Reviews primary text says "safe haven", "173 five-star reviews" (image says 189, real count 231) and "limited spots". Editing the winner's copy can reset its learning.
   - AD09, AD15, AD29, AD32 say "photos and videos every day". The offer is one daily video.
   - Several say "our team". Rule: "us".
