## 4. Pre-publish checklist (any "no" sends it back)

1. [ ] It's one of the 3 formats in section 2.
2. [ ] Slide 1 has 8 words or fewer and reads at thumbnail size.
3. [ ] The dog in every image is a real Four Paws Inn dog. AI is used only for text, layout and background.
4. [ ] 1080x1350 (4:5) for feed. Carousels 5 to 7 slides, one idea per slide.
5. [ ] Brand: cream, pink, charcoal, bold serif heading, clean sans body, small logo on the last slide only.
6. [ ] Every fact comes from `fourpawsinn/business-facts.md`. Offer wording: ONE daily video, never photos or "updates all day".
7. [ ] Review quotes are word for word with the first name only, and match today's offer.
8. [ ] The caption's last line is one easy question. The booking line ("Send us your dates") sits right above it. The long "If you're near Miramar..." boilerplate is gone.
9. [ ] No dashes, no "safe", no health claims, no dog counts, no discounts.
10. [ ] The last slide asks for one action: save, comment or send dates. Only one.

## 5. The ratchet

1. Each week, one test across the 4 PM statics. Example: a real dog cutout on slide 1 vs a full photo.
2. Measure at 48 hours: **saves + shares + comments per 100 views** (this is what Instagram rewards; likes don't move reach).
3. The format with the best 7 day average is the baseline. Claude updates section 2. Losers go to the list below.
4. New mistake found? It becomes a checklist line the same day.

**Goal by Nov 20:** 32 statics a month with 50+ combined saves, shares and comments. Today: 3.

## 7. Repeats are fine (Alex, Oct 20)

Repeat what works. New people find the page every day, and nobody tracks the feed that closely. Only rule: **leave big gaps** between the same topic (aim for about 2 weeks), and change the photo or the opening line when a winner comes back.

Topic ideas to rotate when we need fresh ones: parent feelings (guilt, first night away, coming home), how a stay works (first hour, pool time, the daily video), dog types (shy, big, senior, two dogs, cats), real review stories, booking help (questions to ask, what costs extra, holiday dates), seasonal (Thanksgiving, Christmas, spring break).


## 8. Paid static standard: the news report (Alex, Oct 22, 2026)
The best Meta static ever run is a news article style image ("South Florida Pet Report": masthead, category line, big serif headline, two real photos). Alex: "This is the angle and approach we need with our static images." Why it works: it reads like a story, not an ad; the headline says what we are, where, and the price, so it filters out leads who can't afford it.
Rules: headline = what + where + $60 a day + proof (230+ five star reviews). Real photos only. Every fact true. No fake byline ("Staff Reporter"), no date, never a real publication's name. Builds and ad IDs: content/ads/2026-10-22-creative-refresh.md.

**Masthead (Alex, Oct 22):** "The Four Paws Times". The play on words is the fun and keeps it honest: it's our paper, so nobody is misled. Look: vintage 1940s broadsheet, blackletter masthead, aged newsprint, halftone photos.

## 9. Card style carousels (Alex, Oct 23, 2026): the default for carousels and multi page statics
Reference: the goviralish.com "Double Your Reach Challenge" page Alex sent. Recolored to our palette.

The look:
1. Cream page `#FAF7F3`. One white card per slide, 7px charcoal `#353532` border, round corners, hard offset shadow (no blur).
2. Small spaced uppercase eyebrow label with a dot, on a pink `#E6A0A3` bar.
3. Big bold sans headline. One or two key words in serif italic with a pink highlighter bar behind them.
4. Bold lead in sentence, then plain body. Numbered lists for rules or questions.
5. Real photos only, in a bordered box with the same hard shadow. Never redrawn.
6. Last slide: a pink pill button with border and shadow ("Send us your dates"), plus the optional meet and greet note.
7. Footer: FOUR PAWS INN and the page number. "Swipe" on the first two slides.

How to make one (no credits spent):
1. Copy `content/carousels/2026-10-23-five-questions/` to a new dated folder.
2. Put real photos (downscaled under 1 MB) in `photos/`. Edit `slides.json`: `==word==` highlight, `*word*` serif italic, `**word**` bold. Optional sizes: titleSize, listSize, quoteSize.
3. Run: `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node scripts/carousel-cardstyle/render.js content/carousels/<folder>`
4. Check `contact-sheet.png`: no empty half cards, no text under 38px, highlight on one or two words per slide max.
5. Facts and prices only from `fourpawsinn/business-facts.md`. No dashes, no health or safety claims.

Structure that fits the style: hook slide with the best dog photo, a "the truth" setup slide, one question or rule per slide with a photo, a combined slide for the last two, a review quote plus button to close. 6 to 8 slides.
