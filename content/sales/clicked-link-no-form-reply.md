# Reply: lead clicked the booking link but did not finish the form

Use when the alert says "CLICKED BOOKING LINK, NO FORM YET" and the lead already filled the Facebook instant form.
Rule: never re-ask what the FB form already told us (timeline, size, sociability, pricing OK). Ask only for what is missing: dates and pup's name. Stay on text if that is where they are.

## Template
Hi [Name], it's Amanda again 😊 I saw you started our booking page. No need to finish it. Just text me your dates here.

Your pup is under 50 lbs and friendly, so they will fit right in with our play groups. It's $60 a day.

What dates do you need, and what's your pup's name? I'll check our openings right away.

## Swaps
- Over 50 lbs: "$75 a day".
- Puppy or senior: "$80 a day".
- Shy or selective: replace the play group line with "We will help them settle in at their own pace."
