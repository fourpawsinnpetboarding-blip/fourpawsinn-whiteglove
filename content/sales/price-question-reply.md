# Reply: "How much does it cost to board?"

Use for every price question by text. Value first, then price, then one question that moves to booking.
First used Oct 12, 2026 (Sondra Hertz). Facts from fourpawsinn/business-facts.md.

## Text (copy and paste, swap [Name] and her/him)

Hi [Name]! Thanks for reaching out. Your pup stays in our family home in Miramar, not a kennel. Dogs go outside to play every hour, in small groups matched by size and energy. You get photo and video updates every day.

Our price is by the day:
Under 50 lb: $60 a day
Over 50 lb: $75 a day
Puppies and seniors: $80 a day
Second dog: $5 a day off

Pick up before 10 AM and your last day is free. A 25% deposit holds your dates.

What dates do you need, and how much does she weigh? I'll check if we have room.

## Why it works
1. Value before price, so the number lands next to what they get.
2. Every price in one view. No back and forth.
3. Ends with a question, so the lead answers and the sale moves forward.
