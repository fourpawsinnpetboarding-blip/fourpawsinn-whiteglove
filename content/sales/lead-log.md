## What the pattern says (small sample, but clear enough to act on)
1. **The two that booked talked with us first.** Ally and Kirsten shared their dog, asked their own questions, and asked for the price. Price followed their interest.
2. **The ones that stalled got price, deposit and urgency in the same message,** on reply 2 or 3. For a $600+ decision from a stranger, that reads as a sale, not a conversation.
3. **Rachel was a fit problem, not a pushing problem.** No reply would have fixed an 11 PM pick up.

## New reply pacing (replaces "every reply pushes toward deposit")
1. **Reply 1: the dog.** Name, breed, age, weight, how they do with other dogs, dates. Warm and curious. No price unless they asked.
2. **Reply 2: their worry.** Answer the worry they named (shy dog, other dogs, first time boarding). Offer proof: "Want me to send a quick video of the yard and the dogs?" Ask one question about their dog's routine.
3. **Price: when they ask, or once they've engaged about the dog.** Higher number first, the before 10 AM number, value next to it, all 3 payment options. End with a question about the dog or the dates, NOT "want me to hold it?"
4. **Deposit: only after they say yes or ask how to book.** Then: "Great. To hold the dates the deposit is 25%, $X. Zelle, Apple Pay or card, whichever is easiest."
5. **Urgency (holidays filling): only if true, only once, and never in the same message as the first price.**
6. **Comparing or "let me think":** short, warm, no push. One soft check in about 5 to 7 days later with something useful (a video of a dog like theirs, an answer to a common worry), never "just following up".

Exception: a lead who already gives dates, dog details and asks the price in the first message (like Ally) gets the price right away. Match their speed, never get ahead of it.

## "Take your time" reply (Alex, Oct 22, 2026)
For a lead who has the price and is deciding, or for a big stay (holidays, a week or more). Tone: here's how it works, take your time, we're here.

```
Here's how it works with us, so you have everything in one place.

1. You fill out a short form about your dog and sign our boarding agreement.
2. A 25% deposit holds the dates. Zelle, Apple Pay or card.
3. The rest is paid before drop off.

I know this is a big decision, so take the time you need. If you'd like to see the home first, we do meet and greets on Tuesdays. Just let me know.

[Week] is filling up. If it gets close to full, I'll give you a heads up before your dates are gone. No pressure at all.
```

What it changes vs the old way:
- Explains the process instead of asking for the deposit. The lead decides when.
- Offers the Tuesday meet and greet as a way to decide, for leads who are weighing a big stay. Still never required, and never offered as a trial or dog intro.
- Availability becomes a service ("I'll give you a heads up") instead of pressure. Only say a week is filling up when it is.

## Custom plans: no meet and greet yet (Alex, Oct 24, 2026)
When a lead needs a custom plan (daycare askers, tough work schedules, regular weekly stays), build the plan by text first. Give the windows (9 AM to 12 PM, 4 PM to 6:30 PM, 7 days), how billing works (by the day, last day free if picked up before 10 AM), the rate, then ask: which days, work hours (leave and get home), nights in a row or one at a time, the dog's name. Custom plans stay INSIDE our routine (Alex, Oct 24): the windows 9 AM to 12 PM and 4 PM to 6:30 PM never move, no early or late pick ups, and don't promise to work around a lead's schedule. Keep pricing blanket: the day rate only, no talk of late checkouts or the 10 AM rule in custom plan texts. First case: Anton Foresta.

## THE SALES ORDER (Alex, Oct 31, 2026, hard rule for every lead)
"Don't ask for money immediately without asking questions, getting to know the pet. THEN you show our value so the customer knows why they're spending money and what it will be spent on."

2. **Show the value, fitted to what they told us.** What their dog's stay looks like here: a real home, no kennels, outside to play about every hour, supervised pool time, one on one time first and slow intros, a calm house to sleep in, one video update each day, no hidden fees, curbside drop off. 3. **Only then the price.** The full stay total, the before 10 AM pickup rule, the 3 ways to pay, and the optional Tuesday meet and greet.
4. **Booking steps and deposit only after they say yes.**
A reply that jumps to step 3 or 4 is wrong, even if it's accurate.
