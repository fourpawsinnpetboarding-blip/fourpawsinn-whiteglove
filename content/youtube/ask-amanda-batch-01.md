  1. "First time boarding your dog?"

## Part 1: First time boarding (questions 1 to 10)

1. **"It's my dog's first time away from home. Will they be ok?"**
   - So many of our families felt this way.
   - We go slow, and every dog settles in at their own pace.
   - You get photos and videos every day.
2. **"How do you help a new dog settle in?"**
   - First, one on one time with the team.
   - Then quiet time to explore.
   - Then the group slowly, when the dog is ready. New dogs never go straight into the pack.
3. **"Should I do a short stay before a long trip?"**
   - We often suggest one short overnight first.
   - It lets us bond with your pup, so the longer stay feels familiar.
   - It's recommended, never required.
4. **"Can I see the home before I book?"**
   - Yes. Tours are on Tuesdays, 9 AM to 7 PM.
   - The tour is to see the home and ask questions. It's optional, and your dog doesn't meet the pack on a tour.
5. **"Why no tours on weekends?"**
   - Weekends are our busiest days.
   - New visitors can unsettle dogs who just checked in.
6. **"What do I need to book?"**
   - The intake form, then the boarding agreement by email, then a 25% deposit to hold your dates.
   - The rest is paid before drop off.
7. **"What vaccines does my dog need?"**
   - Vaccines must be current.
   - [Amanda lists the exact ones we require.]
8. **"What should I pack?"**
   - Food, pre-portioned in Ziploc bags.
   - We provide the beds and supplies.
   - You'll get our welcome guide with everything else.
9. **"Can I bring their bed or a toy from home?"**
   - [Amanda answers in her own words.]
10. **"Will I get updates while I'm away?"**
    - Yes, photos and videos every day. Show a real update on your phone.

## Part 2: Shy dogs, siblings and the pack (questions 11 to 20)

11. **"My dog doesn't like other dogs. Can they still stay?"**
    - First we ask why. Shy and aggressive are different things.
    - Many shy dogs join the pack after a few hours or a few days.
12. **"What happens if my dog is shy?"**
    - We go slow and read their energy and body language.
    - We decide day by day.
13. **"Do you have private rooms?"**
    - Yes, for dogs who do best on their own.
    - We can start a dog in a private room and ease them into the pack when they're ready.
14. **"I have two dogs. Will they stay together?"**
    - Siblings always stay together here, no matter what.
15. **"What if one of my dogs is social and the other is shy?"**
    - The social one plays with the pack, then comes back inside to their sibling.
    - The shy one can go outside with their sibling.
16. **"Do you take every dog?"**
    - Not every dog. A big dog showing signs of aggression doesn't join the group.
    - That protects every family's pet.
17. **"Why do you keep groups small?"**
    - On purpose, so shy and selective dogs feel calm, and so the team builds a one on one bond with every dog.
    - Don't give a number.
18. **"How do you know when a dog is ready for the group?"**
    - [Amanda describes the signs she looks for, in her own words.]
19. **"Do you take puppies and senior dogs?"**
    - Yes. [Amanda shares how their days look different.]
20. **"Do you take cats?"**
    - Yes. Cats get their own private room with a window to the yard and a bird feeder outside.
    - We provide the litter box, toys, cat posts and beds.

## Part 3: How a stay works (questions 21 to 30)

21. **"Is it cage free? Where do the dogs sleep?"**
    - Yes, it's a real home.
    - They play in the yard and sleep indoors. Show the room.
22. **"What does a day look like here?"**
    - Dogs go outside to play every hour.
    - [Amanda walks through morning to night.]
23. **"Do the dogs go in the pool?"**
    - Yes, supervised pool time. Show it.
24. **"Do the dogs sleep outside?"**
    - No, they sleep indoors. A lead asked us this exact question this month.
25. **"How do drop off and pick up work?"**
    - Curbside. Text us when you're on the way.
    - We bring your pet to your car in about 20 seconds.
26. **"What are your drop off times?"**
    - 9 AM to 12 PM, and 4 PM to 6:30 PM, every day.
27. **"How does pricing work?"**
    - We charge by the day, not by the night.
    - No hidden fees: the price you see is the price you pay.
    - No extra charges for play time, toys or baths.
28. **"Why is the last day free if I pick up early?"**
    - Pick up before 10 AM and your last day isn't charged. That's just how our billing works.
    - Don't call it a deal.
29. **"What makes Four Paws Inn different from a kennel?"**
    - A real home, no cages, small groups, slow intros, and daily updates.
    - Don't name a competitor.
30. **"Why did you start Four Paws Inn?"**
    - Amanda's own story, in her words. This one is filmed last because it's the warmest and makes the best long video ending.
