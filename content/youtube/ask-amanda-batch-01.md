# Ask Amanda, batch 01 (film Oct 20, 2026)

Format: Amanda talking head in the house (Insta360 or phone). Each answer is 60 to 120 seconds, filmed as one take. Cut each into a YouTube Short, plus one 6 to 8 minute long video ("First time boarding your dog? 5 questions answered").
Channel: Four Paws Inn YouTube (business workspace). Questions come from real lead texts this month.
Rules: talking points, not a script. No "safe", no health promises, no dog counts, no prices except where noted.

## 1. "It's my dog's first time away from home. Will they be ok?"
- Very common. Most of our families felt the same way.
- We go slow. Each dog settles in at their own pace.
- Photos and videos every day so you can see how they're doing.
- Many families start with one short overnight before a long trip.

## 2. "My dog doesn't like other dogs. Can they still stay?"
- Ask why first. Shy and aggressive are different.
- Many shy dogs join the pack after a few hours or a few days.
- We read each dog's energy and body language, and decide day by day.
- Private rooms for dogs who do best on their own.
- A big dog showing aggression doesn't join the group. That protects every family's pet.

## 3. "I have two dogs. Can they stay together?"
- Siblings always stay together here. No matter what.
- If one loves to play and one is shy, the social one plays with the pack and comes back inside to their sibling.

## 4. "Is it cage free? Where do they sleep?"
- Yes. A real home. A big yard and a pool for play, then the dogs sleep indoors.
- Show the room on camera.

## 5. "How do drop off and pick up work?"
- Curbside. Text us when you're on the way, and we bring your pet to your car.
- After you book, you get our welcome guide with everything to bring.

End line for every video: "If you're near Miramar and need boarding, send us your dates."
