# Pack Member Program: offer and money model (DRAFT, Alex to approve)

Built Oct 10 2026 from Alex's brief, using the money model structure from Alex Hormozi's $100M Money Models: attraction offer, core offer, upsell, downsell, continuity.
Rules: no health, safety or training result guarantees in any copy. Describe what is included and the process. Client facing copy at a 3rd grade reading level, no dashes.

## The big idea (Alex's words, cleaned up)

Your dog stays at the best rated home boarding in the area, at your normal rate, while you travel. Add training to the same stay. Your dog comes home with new manners, and becomes a Four Paws Inn Pack Member for life: first pick of dates, holiday stays at the normal rate, and member only deals. A trained dog is a better guest for us, so we want them back, and we reward that.

## 1. Core offer: Pack Member Board and Train (2 weeks)

**Price frame:** your normal boarding rate plus **$175 a day** for training.
| Dog | Boarding | Training | Per day | 14 days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 50 lb | $60 | $175 | $235 | **$3,290** |
| Over 50 lb | $75 | $175 | $250 | **$3,500** |
| Puppy | $80 | $175 | $255 | **$3,570** |
Founding families (first 5): training at $150 a day instead of $175.

**What is included:**
1. 14 days of cage free home boarding, a daily video update.
2. 3 short training sessions a day on 5 skills: loose leash walking, sit and down with a stay, come when called, place, polite greetings.
3. A 1 hour go home lesson so you know the commands.
4. Written notes on every command.
5. 2 follow up check ins (2 weeks and 30 days after).
6. **Pack Member status for 12 months** (below).

**Who it is for:** dogs who pull, jump, or do not come when called, and puppies 16 weeks and older with current vaccines. Not for bite history or aggression.

## 2. Pack Member perks (the reason they come back)

For 12 months after graduation:
1. **Priority booking:** members book holiday dates before we open them to the public.
2. **Holiday rate lock:** holiday stays at the normal daily rate. Non members pay +$15 a day on peak holidays.
3. **Refresher sessions:** one free training refresher on every future stay.
4. **Member deals:** every 10th night free, and a free night on the dog's birthday stay.
5. **Pack Member badge** on the dog's profile and a graduation photo for the owner.

Protect capacity: priority booking means members book first, not unlimited spots. Holiday spots for members are held only when booked at least 30 days ahead. Never promise "we will always have a spot" in copy.

## 3. Upsell: Holiday Package (sell at the same time as the core offer)

Pair the training with the client's real trip.
1. Book Board and Train during your Thanksgiving, Christmas or New Year trip.
2. **Bank future nights now at today's rate:** 10 future nights prepaid, usable within 12 months, at the normal rate even on holidays.
3. Example, under 50 lb dog: $3,290 training stay + 10 banked nights at $60 = $600. Total $3,890 today. Cash up front, and the client comes back to use the nights.

## 4. Downsell: Training Add On (for clients who say no to the full program)

Add training to any regular stay: **+$100 a day**, 2 sessions a day on 2 skills of their choice. Minimum 5 days. No Pack Member status (that stays the reason to upgrade). If they like it, credit what they paid toward the full program within 60 days.

## 5. Attraction offers (lead magnets)

**For active clients (no ad spend):**
1. **Free Manners Report Card on every stay:** during any boarding stay we score the dog on the 5 skills and send the owner a short report with photos. Every report ends with the Pack Member offer. This turns the $20K to $30K a month boarding base into a training lead machine.
2. **Waitlist text** to past clients (founding price, first 5 families).

**For new leads (Meta ads, picture ad):**
1. Picture ad: "Going away for the holidays? Your dog stays in the best rated home in Miramar, and comes home with better manners." Button: Join the training waitlist.
2. Lead magnet: **"The 5 Manners Every Dog Needs Before Your Next Trip"** one page checklist, sent by text after the form. Then the GHL follow up offers the Pack Member program.
3. Form questions (training form only): dog's name, age, size, top problem (pulls, jumps, does not come, puppy manners), next trip date.

## 6. The money model in one line

Free Manners Report Card (attraction) -> Pack Member Board and Train, $3,290 to $3,570 (core) -> Holiday Package with banked nights (upsell) -> Training Add On at $100 a day (downsell) -> 12 months of Pack Member perks and refreshers, which bring them back (continuity).

## 7. Goal math

10 programs a month at about $3,400 average = about $34,000, plus boarding. Banked nights add cash up front. Pack Members fill holidays at the normal rate first.

## Decisions (Claude recommended, Alex said "what you think is best", Oct 10 2026)
1. Training add on: **$175 a day.** Round number, lands the over 50 lb dog at exactly $3,500, premium position.
2. Holiday rate: **+$15 a day for non members** on peak holidays (Thanksgiving week, Dec 20 to Jan 3, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day weekends). Pack Members pay the normal rate. Needs Alex's final yes before it goes in any copy, since it changes pricing for every client.
3. Minimum puppy age: **16 weeks**, after the core puppy shots. Vaccines must be current (existing rule).
4. Member deals: **every 10th night free** plus **a free night on the dog's birthday stay.** Simple, cheap, drives repeat stays. Replaces "buy 3 get 1".
5. Copy rules stand: no training result promises, no "always a spot" promise.
