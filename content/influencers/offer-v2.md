# Influencer offer v2: Partner Program (draft, needs Alex's yes)

Alex, Oct 11: "A free two night stay might not be enticing. We can pay, give a free stay, or give them an offer where every person they get they can make income."

## The problem with v1
A free 2 night stay is worth $120 to a creator with a dog, and $0 to a creator without one (moms, local businesses, most of the list). There is no upside if their post works. Creators say yes to offers that pay more when they perform.

## The offer (recommended: Option A)

| Piece | What they get | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Earn per family | $50 for every new family that books with their code, paid after that family's stay ends | Everyone |
| 2. Earn for a year | 10% of every stay that family books for 12 months | Everyone |
| 3. Free stay | 3 nights free for their own pet, on open dates, not holidays | Creators with a pet |
| 4. Their followers save | $25 off the first stay with the creator's code | Everyone |
| 5. Flat fee (rare) | $150 for 1 reel plus 3 stories | Only creators with 10K+ local followers, Alex approves each one |

Option B (more cash up front): $100 to $250 flat per reel plus $50 per booking, no 12 month share. Pays for posts, not results. Use only if A gets no yeses after 30 sends.

## The math (Option A)
Example family: first stay $300 (Mia's booking this week), books 3 times a year.
- Year 1 revenue: $900.
- Cost: $25 discount, $50 first booking, $60 (10% of the 2 repeat stays) = $135.
- Four Paws Inn keeps $765 in revenue, about $465 profit at our ~67% margin (profit divided by revenue).
- CAC (cost to get one new paying customer): $75 on the first stay. If a creator brings nobody, cost is $0, or $120 of empty nights for a pet stay.
- One creator who brings 5 families earns $250 to $550 in a year. That is the pitch: real income, no cap.

## Rules
1. Paid only on completed stays from new families. Their code is on the booking.
2. Payout on the 1st of each month by Zelle or PayPal. W-9 (IRS tax form with their name and tax ID) collected before the first payout.
3. Every post is marked as an ad (Instagram "Paid partnership" tag or #ad). FTC rule (the federal rule that paid posts must say they are paid).
4. We never pay for Google reviews or ask creators for one.
5. No health or safety claims in their posts. We send them the facts sheet.
6. Codes: creator name plus 25 (example: TEDDY25).
