## 1. "Any recommendations for dog boarding near Pembroke Pines / Miramar?"
Full disclosure, my wife and I own Four Paws Inn in west Miramar, so I'm biased! We're a cage free home, not a kennel. Dogs go outside to play every hour in small groups by size and energy, and you get photo and video updates every day. $60 a day under 50 lb. Happy to answer any questions, and if we're not the right fit for your pup I'll tell you.

## 2. "My dog is anxious / doesn't do well in kennels. Where should I board?"
I own a boarding home in Miramar (Four Paws Inn), so take this with that in mind. A few things to ask any place you look at: where does my dog sleep, how do new dogs meet the group, and how will I know how they're doing? For nervous dogs a slow start helps a lot. We let new dogs explore the house first, then the yard, then the group when they're ready. Feel free to message me with questions, even if you go somewhere else.
