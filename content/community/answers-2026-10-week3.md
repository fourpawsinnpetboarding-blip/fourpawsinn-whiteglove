## 1. "Anyone know a boarder that takes two dogs together?"
I own Four Paws Inn in Miramar, so I'm biased! Most good places will. Ask if they keep siblings together the whole stay. We always do, even when one dog is social and the other is shy. The social one plays with the group and comes back to their sibling. Ask about the price for a second dog too. Ours is $5 a day less.

## 2. "My dog is reactive. Is boarding even an option?"
Owner of a boarding home in Miramar here. It can be, so be honest with the place up front. Ask how they introduce new dogs and whether they have private space. We start new dogs one on one, and we have private suites for dogs who do best alone. A good place will ask you a lot of questions first. If they don't, keep looking.

## 3. "Does anyone board rabbits or birds in Broward?"
I own Four Paws Inn in Miramar and we do: rabbits, birds, reptiles and small pets. Every pet needs a different setup, so call around and ask what they need from you before the stay. We confirm the price after we learn about your pet. Happy to answer questions.
