# Community answers, week of Oct 19 (3x plan row 7)

Owner posts: Alex or Amanda, from their own account. Always say you own Four Paws Inn. Only answer real posts asking for boarding help. Never post fake reviews or ask friends to pose as customers. No price fights with other businesses.
Where: Nextdoor (Miramar, Pembroke Pines, Weston, Cooper City, Davie), local Facebook groups, Reddit r/fortlauderdale or r/Miami when someone asks.

## 1. "Need dog boarding for Thanksgiving, is it too late?"
I own Four Paws Inn in west Miramar, so I'm biased! It's not too late, but holiday weeks fill first everywhere, so I'd lock something in this week. Most places hold holiday dates with a deposit (ours is 25%). If your dog has never boarded with a place before, a short overnight first can help. We suggest one for shy pups, so the holiday stay isn't their first time with us. (Edited Oct 20: the required first overnight is retired.)

## 2. "Dog sitter at home or boarding? Which is better?"
Owner of a boarding home in Miramar here, so keep that in mind. Honestly it depends on your dog. Sitters are great for dogs who love their own space and routine. Boarding is great for dogs who like company and play. Ask any option how often your dog goes outside, who is there overnight, and how you'll get updates. Happy to answer questions either way.

## 3. "My dog is picky with other dogs. Will a boarding place even take him?"
I own Four Paws Inn in Miramar. A lot of good places will, so don't give up. Ask how they introduce new dogs. We have a careful intro process and always take it slow, and we have a private suite for dogs who do best on their own. Tell them up front how your dog does so they can plan. Feel free to message me.
