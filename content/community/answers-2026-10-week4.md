## 1. "First time boarding my dog. What should I ask?"
I own Four Paws Inn in Miramar, so I'm biased, but here is what I would ask anywhere. How do new dogs meet the group? How often do dogs go outside? What updates will I get, and how often? Is there anything that costs extra? We start new dogs one on one, they go out about every hour, and you get one video update each day. A good place will ask you a lot about your dog too.

## 2. "Need a boarder for a big dog, 80 lb. Ideas?"
Owner of a boarding home in Miramar here. Ask how much room they have and how they match dogs by size. We have a big backyard and we look at size, energy and play style before any group time. Our price for dogs over 50 lb is $75 a day. Happy to answer questions.

## 3. "Is it too late to book boarding for Thanksgiving?"
I own Four Paws Inn in Miramar. Not too late to ask! Send your exact dates to a few places now so you can compare. Ask what the deposit is and what is included. Ours is a 25% deposit to hold your dates, and the rest is paid before drop off.
