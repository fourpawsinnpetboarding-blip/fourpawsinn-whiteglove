## 1. "Sitter at home or boarding? Which is better?"
I own Four Paws Inn in Miramar, so I'm biased, but both can work. Ask yourself one thing: how many hours a day will someone be with your dog? A sitter often stops by a few times a day. A boarding home has someone there all day and night. Calm dogs often do great at home. Dogs who get bored alone usually like having friends around.

## 2. "Need boarding this week, last minute. Help!"
Owner of a boarding home in Miramar here. To move fast, send places your dates, your dog's breed, age and weight, how they do with other dogs, and a photo of their vaccine records. That way they can tell you yes or no right away. Happy to check our calendar too.

## 3. "My dog is shy. Will boarding stress them out?"
I own Four Paws Inn in Miramar. Ask any place how they start a shy dog. We go slow: one on one time first, then quiet time to explore. Group time only comes when the dog is ready, and some dogs do best with their own space. Tell them everything about your dog. The more they know, the better they can plan.
