## Substack post

**Title:** My ads ran for months and Facebook never knew who actually booked

**Subtitle:** Founder's Log 01: the numbers, one win, one expensive mistake, and the rule I made from it

I run a home based dog boarding business in Miramar, Florida with my wife Amanda. I am not a developer. I run it with two AIs, Claude and Codex, that leave each other notes on a shared board.

This is the first entry of a weekly log. Real numbers, what worked, what broke.

**The numbers**

57 leads came in from our Facebook and Instagram forms in the last 30 days.
Until October 8, the number of bookings we reported back to Meta was zero.
Since October 8: 14.
One software bill I paid twice: 7 months, $689.

**The win**

Meta finally knows who pays us.

When someone books, we mark it Won in our CRM. That now sends a signal to Meta automatically: this person became a customer. Before, Meta only knew who filled out a form. It was finding me more form fillers, not more customers.

Then we took the typing away from Amanda. Our booking sheet used to need about 10 fields per booking. Now she marks the deposit as paid on the intake form, the booking row fills itself in, and she types one number: the daily rate. The sheet sends the booking to the CRM, and the CRM tells Meta.

**The mistake**

While I was updating a card, I found two accounts with the same name, same login, same company. One was the account we use every day. The other was empty, with zero activity.

The empty one had been charging a different card for 7 months. $689 for nothing. I never noticed because the account looked identical and the card was one I never check.

**The lesson**

Once a month, search every card for every software name, and count the charges. If you count more charges than tools you actually use, you are paying twice.

And the bigger one: an ad platform can only learn from what you tell it. If you only tell it who filled out a form, it will find you more people who fill out forms.

**Next week**

I am opening a waitlist for a new training program. No trainer hired yet. I will show you how many people join, and what I do with that number.

Run a pet business? Reply and tell me your biggest headache. I read every one.
