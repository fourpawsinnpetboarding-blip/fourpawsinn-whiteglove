# Founder's Log 01

**Title:** I run my dog boarding business with two AIs that talk to each other

**Subtitle:** Week 1 numbers, one win, one mess, and the rule that fixed it

---

I am not a developer. I run a home based dog boarding business in Miramar, Florida with my wife Amanda.

This week I gave two AIs real jobs. Claude and ChatGPT's Codex. They leave each other notes on a shared board so I do not have to pass messages between them.

**The numbers**

About $30,000 a month in revenue. About $20,000 in profit. Room for 60 dogs. 215 Google reviews.

The house can hold about four times the business it does today. My problem is not space. It is getting found.

**The win**

Codex went through our CRM and moved 24 dead automations into an archive. Claude is rewriting the three that actually talk to new leads. I did not open a single one.

**The mess**

Codex lost access to the CRM for three days. An error code I did not understand. I spent a full afternoon on support tickets and screenshots. The fix was boring: Codex logs in through a browser like a person does.

**The lesson**

One job, one owner. When both AIs touched the same thing, they stepped on each other. Now one owns the CRM, one owns the copy, and the board is the only place they talk.

**Next week**

I am launching a $3,000 Board and Train program without adding a single kennel. I will show you the math and whether anyone buys.
