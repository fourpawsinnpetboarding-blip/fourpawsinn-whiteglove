Approved by Alex, Oct 7, 2026. Expanded Oct 11, 2026: Substack becomes the Founder's Log, plus a weekly YouTube video.

## Where it goes (personal workspace in Eden, never the Four Paws Inn workspace)
- **Substack:** the weekly Founder's Log post, Monday 9 PM ET, in Codex's existing Substack slot. Codex posts it. Codex's other Substack posts that week follow the same voice: build in public, real numbers, no hype.

- **YouTube (personal channel):** one talking head video a week. Alex films Sunday with the teleprompter (script in the same file), Claude edits with the youtube-long-edit skill, Alex approves before it goes up.

## Format (every entry)
Hook, numbers, one win, one mistake, the rule (SOP lesson), next week cliffhanger, and one ask: "Run a pet business? Reply and tell me your biggest headache."

## Rules
- Only verified numbers. Revenue and profit stay out unless Alex says yes for that entry.
- No client names, no dog health claims, no dashes, no hype.
- No claims about capacity (no "60 dogs").
- Nothing is "launching" until it has a date and a person behind it.

## Signal
10 business owners reaching out on their own moves Step 2 up. Launch rule for a paid offer (Claude, Oct 11): FPI at $60K a month for 3 months in a row, Board and Train at 10 a month, and 50+ owners raising their hand.
