# Board and Train trainer scorecard (Oct 13, 2026)

Use it to pick one trainer. Score every candidate the same way. Terms: `trainer-post.md` (flat fee per dog, no split, total trainer cost at most 30% of $3,000 = $900 or less per program).

## Must haves (any "no" = out)
1. Own liability insurance, or will get it before the first dog.
2. Accepts a flat fee per dog. No revenue split.
3. Will sign: no soliciting our clients for 2 years, we own photos, videos and training plans.
4. Reward based, calm methods. No shock or prong collars.
5. Not running a competing board and train in Broward.

## Score 1 to 5
| Area | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| Proof | 3+ before and after videos, real dogs, real progress |
| References | 2 past clients answer and say they would hire again |
| Rate | $900 or less per 21 day program |
| Availability | Can start a first dog within 2 weeks, and can take 3 a month |
| Owner handoff | Clear plan for the pick up lesson, so owners keep the results |
| Fit with Amanda | Amanda would trust them alone with client dogs |
