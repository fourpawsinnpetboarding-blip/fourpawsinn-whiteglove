# Board and Train trainer: hiring post and terms (draft, Alex approves before posting)

## Terms (Alex decides the numbers)
1. Contractor, paid a flat fee per dog per 21 day program. Not a revenue split.
2. Four Paws Inn owns the client, the price ($3,000), the brand, the reviews and the program.
3. Written agreement before the first dog: scope, flat fee, payment after each program, the trainer's own liability insurance, no soliciting Four Paws Inn clients for 2 years, Four Paws Inn owns all photos, videos and training plans.
4. Paid trial: 1 dog first. Alex and Amanda judge the result before a second dog.
5. Flat fee range to test: market check needed. Ask each candidate their rate for a 21 day program. Keep total trainer cost at or under 30% of the $3,000 price so each program keeps $2,100 or more.

## The post

Title: Dog Trainer, Board and Train Program (Miramar, FL)

Four Paws Inn is a family run, cage free dog boarding home in Miramar with 230+ Google reviews.

We are adding a 21 day Board and Train program. We want one great trainer to run it with us.

What you do:
1. Train each dog for 21 days at our home in Miramar.
2. Teach the basics: sit, down, stay, come, loose leash walking, and calm manners.
3. Film short clips of each dog's progress for the owner.
4. Meet the owner at pick up and show them how to keep it going.

What we want:
1. Real results you can show us, with videos.
2. Calm, kind, reward based training.
3. Your own liability insurance.
4. A trainer who shows up on time, every time.

What you get:
1. A flat fee for every dog. We find the clients. You train.
2. A steady flow of dogs from our boarding clients.
3. A clean, safe home to work in, with a team that cares.

To apply, send us:
1. Your rate for one 21 day program.
2. 3 videos of dogs you trained.
3. 2 references from past clients.

Email: fourpawsinnpetboarding@gmail.com
