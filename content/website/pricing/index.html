FAQs

How do you charge?

By the day. Every day your dog is with us counts, drop off day and pick up day too. If you pick up before 10 AM, the last day is free.

When can I drop off and pick up?

9 AM to 12 PM, or 4 PM to 6:30 PM, 7 days a week.

Do I need a meet and greet first?

No. It is totally optional. If you want to see the home first, we do meet and greets on Tuesdays by appointment.

How do I book?

Fill out our guest form, sign our agreement by email, and pay a 25% deposit to hold your dates. The rest is paid before drop off.

What vaccines does my dog need?

Vaccines must be current. Send the records anytime and we keep them on file.

What should I bring?

We send you our welcome guide after you book. It covers what to bring and how drop off works.
